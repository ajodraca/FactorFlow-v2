# Tasks: CAP-002-E2-S3 — Value and Growth Inputs from Quarterly Fundamentals

**Story**: `docs/capabilities/CAP-002-universe-and-factor-classification/epics/automatic-factor-classification-engine/stories/CAP-002-E2-S3.md`
**Component**: `FactorFlow-v2`
**Branch**: `usr/alvaro-de-jodra/CAP-002-E2-tasks`
**Date**: 2026-10-17

## Summary

Adds the fundamentals stage of the factor engine: `statements.sql` (latest observation per quarterly period and statement type, with the backfill flag column and the TTM and prior-year window slots), `value_inputs.sql` (`book_to_price`, `earnings_yield`, `fcf_yield`), `growth_inputs.sql` (`revenue_growth`, `eps_growth`) and `insert_inputs.sql` (the `factor_input` writer, parameterised by factor so CAP-002-E2-S4 reuses it for momentum). One stage, registered in `engine._STAGES`, creates the temporary tables `stmt` and `inputs`, derives the five inputs for every active instrument with `status`, `value`, `as_of`, `known_on` and `detail`, applies the fundamentals-side currency rule (latest period end more than 6 months before the classification date is `outdated`) and the price rule for value inputs (latest bar precedes the latest trading day held is `outdated`), and writes the rows. Line-item payload keys come from `line_items.LINE_ITEMS`; while a key is `REQUIRES_INPUT` the dependent inputs are `missing` with `detail.cause = line_item_mapping_unresolved`, and the tests declare their own keys. Seven mapped test cases (TC-COMP-060, 061, 062, 094, 100, 101, 161) are implemented, plus a supporting case for TC-COMP-059 showing that unresolved line-item keys give `missing` value and growth inputs. The "momentum still classifies" clause needs every stage and is asserted by CAP-002-E2-S7 T014, appended to this story's `tests/integration/test_engine_inputs_fundamentals.py`. No momentum input, peer set, classification or restatement mark is written here.

### Resolved Conflicts

- **S3 versus S6 on the backfill flag.** S3's description gives `statements.sql` "the backfill flag column"; S6 "wires the backfill flag from `statements.sql` through `factor_input` into `factor_classification.restated_possible`", while design 2.4 has `restated_possible` as a column of `inputs`. S3 computes `backfilled` (DEC-0056) and reports it in `detail`; `inputs.restated_possible` is written `false`; S6 turns that into the mark. No design change needed.

A stock whose latest bar precedes `L` has no `close(L)`, and DEC-0055 (5) and DAC-E2-6 make it `outdated`. Both read together without contradiction: the stock's own latest bar close is used for market capitalisation, and the stale-bar rule decides the status. Established by DEC-0055 (5), (6) and DAC-E2-S3-6. No design change needed.
