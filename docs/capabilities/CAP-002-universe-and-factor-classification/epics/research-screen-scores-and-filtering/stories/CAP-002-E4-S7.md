# Selection Tray and Inspector Panel

## User Story

As an owner-operator, I want a tray for checked stocks with a Compare action and an inspector for the selected stock, so that I can compare stocks and see one stock's inputs without leaving the list.

## Description

Delivers `web/src/components/patterns/selection-tray.tsx` and `inspector-panel.tsx`. The tray appears at one checked row, announces {n} selected, and **Compare side by side** is `blocked` with its reason at 1 and at 7 checked and enabled at 2 to 6, routing to `/research/compare?symbols=`. The inspector shows memberships (links or In no universe), per factor percentile, signed standardised score and the inputs' `as_of` and `known_on`, the explanation sentence, and a link to every input and its date.

Source: `component-design-classification-ui.md` sections 2.5, 7.

## Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)

### From Capability / EPIC

- [AC-16] The user MUST be able to sort stocks by any factor's score or percentile, filter them with thresholds on one or more factors at once, and view chosen stocks side by side, both across all classified stocks and within a single universe. (the sort, filter and selection controls)

### Derived

- [DAC-E4-S7-1] Given 1 or 7 checked stocks, When the tray renders, Then Compare is `blocked` with its reason and stays focusable; at 2 to 6 it is enabled. (traces to: AC-16)
- [DAC-E4-S7-2] Given a selected stock, When the inspector loads, Then one read is issued after a 150 ms debounce and shows each input with `as_of` and `known_on`. (traces to: AC-16)
- [DAC-E4-S7-3] Given nothing selected, When the inspector renders, Then the empty sentence is shown. (traces to: AC-16)
- [DAC-E4-S7-4] Given a stock in no active universe, When the inspector renders, Then it reads In no universe. (traces to: AC-16)
