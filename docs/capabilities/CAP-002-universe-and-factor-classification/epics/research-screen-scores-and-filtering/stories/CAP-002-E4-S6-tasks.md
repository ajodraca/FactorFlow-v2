# Tasks: CAP-002-E4-S6 — Virtualised Results Table with Server-Side Sort and Selection

**Story**: `repos/factorflow-v2/FactorFlow-v2/docs/capabilities/CAP-002-universe-and-factor-classification/epics/research-screen-scores-and-filtering/stories/CAP-002-E4-S6.md`

**Date**: 2026-10-17

## Summary

Delivers `web/src/screens/research/results-table.tsx`, the one table composition behind the Research screen's Results pane and a universe's Members tab, plus a small pure windowing module `results-window.ts`. The table is a native `<table>` windowed over keyset pages (hand-written, no new dependency): the DOM holds a fixed number of rows however many stocks match, and more pages are appended as the window nears the loaded end. Sorting is a request: each sortable header issues the list read with `sort` and `order` through the controlled `ResearchQuery`, `aria-sort` follows, focus stays, and the new match count is announced politely; the table never sorts, filters or counts a loaded page. Each factor cell is S3's `FactorCell`; a factor below 30 qualifying stocks shows *{n} qualified, 30 needed* once in its header, *Not enough to rank* in every cell and no sort control; a stock incomplete on one factor reads two percentiles and one *Incomplete*, and the header says incomplete rows sort last. Selection (click or `Enter`, `aria-selected`, `↑`/`↓` with a 150 ms debounced `onInspect` notification) and checking (checkbox, `Space`) are independent, and both are controlled by the host by symbol so they survive sort, filter, scope and the refresh-once. The screen (E4-S9), the tray and inspector (E4-S7), the filters pane (E4-S5) and the Members tab (E1-S8) compose it; none is built here.

The `"no-match"` return is the body's only row under the kept header (T011).

- **Risk.** The largest risk is focus and the pending-focus effect under windowing: an arrow press that moves the selection to a row outside the rendered window must scroll first and focus after the row renders.
