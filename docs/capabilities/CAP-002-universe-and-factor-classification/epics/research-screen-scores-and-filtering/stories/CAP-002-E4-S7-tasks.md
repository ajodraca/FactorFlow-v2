# Tasks: CAP-002-E4-S7 — Selection Tray and Inspector Panel

**Story**: `repos/factorflow-v2/FactorFlow-v2/docs/capabilities/CAP-002-universe-and-factor-classification/epics/research-screen-scores-and-filtering/stories/CAP-002-E4-S7.md`

## Summary

Delivers the two panes beside the Research results table: `web/src/components/patterns/selection-tray.tsx` and `web/src/components/patterns/inspector-panel.tsx`. The tray is a controlled, stateless `role="region"` labelled *Selection* that appears at one checked row, announces *{n} selected* politely, and renders **Compare side by side** as a link to `/research/compare?symbols=` at 2 to 6 checked and as a focusable `blocked` button carrying *Check 2 to 6 stocks to compare them.* at 1 and at 7 or more (DEC-0070). The inspector is given one symbol, waits 150 ms for it to settle, issues exactly one `getStockEvidence` read, and shows the memberships line (links or *In no universe*), one block per factor (percentile and mark, signed standardised score, and the inputs with `as_of` and `known_on`), the *Incomplete* explanation box, **See every input and its date**, and the footnote; with no symbol it shows the empty sentence. Both are patterns reused by Research and a universe's Members tab. No screen, table, dialog or route is built here; CAP-002-E4-S9 composes them on the Research screen, and no story yet mounts them on a universe's Members tab (see Open Gaps, *Members-tab mount unassigned*). Three prerequisites (CAP-002-E4-S1, CAP-002-E4-S2 and CAP-002-E4-S3) must be on the branch and are read before coding: S1 (`getStockEvidence`, `StockEvidence`), S2 (`format.ts`, `copy.ts`) and S3 (`FactorCell`, `RestatedMark`).
