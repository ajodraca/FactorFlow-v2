# Tasks: CAP-002-E1-S9 — Universe Detail Screen: Summary Header, Rename, Add Stocks and Retire

**Story**: `docs/capabilities/CAP-002-universe-and-factor-classification/epics/universe-definition-and-maintenance/stories/CAP-002-E1-S9.md`
**Component**: `FactorFlow-v2` (module `classification-ui`, universe detail screen)
**Branch**: `usr/alvaro-de-jodra/CAP-002-E1-tasks`
**Date**: 2026-10-17

## Summary

Build the `UniverseDetailScreen` of the Research area under `web/src/screens/research/universes/detail/`: `index.tsx` (default export; reads `:universeId`, the page `h1`, breadcrumb, the two tabs with the active tab and `as_of` in the URL, and the dialogs), `summary-header.tsx` (the `card` `summary` with inline rename, Lifecycle, Members, Created, Origin, **Add stocks…** and **Retire…**), `add-stocks-dialog.tsx` and `retire-dialog.tsx`. It mounts `MembersTab` and `HistoryTab` and opens the retire dialog from the `onRetire` of CAP-002-E1-S8's Remove dialog, and it supplies the host behaviour CAP-002-E1-S8's plan states as its contract: `as_of` and the tab live in the URL, `HistoryTab` is mounted fresh each time its tab is opened, the header is re-read on `onMembershipChanged`. Rename sends `PATCH`, Add stocks `POST .../members`, Retire `DELETE /api/universes/{id}` through CAP-002-E4-S1's fetch layer; strings come from CAP-002-E4-S2's `copy.ts`. A retired universe keeps both tabs readable, offers no write, and shows the `universe_retired` sentence if a stale page attempts one. The story registers no route (CAP-002-E4-S10) and builds no members table or history table (CAP-002-E1-S8).

## Notes

### Resolved Conflicts

- **Confirmation by toast.** UX design 1.4 and classification-ui 2.7 confirm renames-adjacent writes (add, retire) by `toast` `success`; no toast component exists and none is in any dependency. Following CAP-002-E1-S8's DEC-0107, the text is delivered as a polite `role="status"` line (DEC-0109). Not a design defect: the toast pattern is simply unbuilt.
- **Wording "attempted write" on a retired universe (DAC-E1-S9-5).** A retired universe offers no write control, so the `universe_retired` sentence is reachable only from a stale page that attempted a write before learning of the retirement (or from CAP-002-E1-S8's Remove action, covered by its own plan). The story AC governs over any reading that would keep a disabled write control on the retired page; the plan tests the stale-page case (T009).
- **Rename on a retired universe vs. inline rename.** UX design 1.4 renders the name as an inline rename on the header; the same section says that on a retired universe both header actions are absent. The retired header therefore has no rename control (AC-4's write refusal, DAC-E1-7). No source edit required.
- **Stale self-labels.** Both approved component designs consulted say `**Status:** draft` in the body while front matter is `approved`; front matter governs. No behavioural effect.
