# Compare, Stock Evidence and Insufficient-Evidence Diagnosis

**EPIC ID:** CAP-002-E5
**Jira Key:** N/A
**Capability:** CAP-002
**Summary:** Let the owner place 2 to 6 stocks side by side without losing one that is incomplete, open any classification to see every input and the date it was known, and find out exactly why a stock is not classified on a factor and what, if anything, recovers it, so a result the owner cannot explain never passes for one they can. Delivers the Compare and Stock evidence pages.
**Actors:** Owner-operator, classification-ui (Compare and Stock evidence pages), classification-api, classification-reader
**Estimate:** M
**Out of Scope:** The Research list itself and its filters (E4); writing or maintaining universes (E1); computing the classification (E2).
**Dependencies:** E3 (`GET /api/classifications` with an explicit `symbols` list; `GET /api/classifications/stocks/{symbol}` and `/history`). E4 (the shell-composition seam that registers this EPIC's `/research/compare` and `/research/stocks/:symbol` routes, done once by E4's composition story).
**Links:** system-design.md section 3.3 steps 16-17; component-design-classification-ui.md section 2.5 (`comparison-table.tsx`), the Stock evidence mapping in section 10; ux-design.md sections 1.5, 1.6.

## Goals

- Place 2 to 6 checked stocks side by side with every factor and, for one incomplete on a factor, its reason rather than a dropped column.
- Open any stock's evidence to see every input, its value, the date it was known, and the restatement mark where it applies.
- Name the specific missing or outdated input behind an incomplete factor, and the recovery path when one exists.
- Show a stock's earlier classifications exactly as recorded, distinct from never having been scored.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **Completeness under comparison (AC-16, STD-UI-16):** an incomplete or unranked stock keeps its column; nothing is silently dropped.
- **Accessibility:** the comparison table's row and column headers are programmatic; the per-input dates disclosure is keyboard-operable.
- **Honesty (STD-UI-18):** the statement that absence judges presence and currency, not provider correctness, appears once per screen.

## EPIC Acceptance Criteria

### From Capability

- [AC-13] An input MUST be treated as outdated when it is a fundamental whose latest reported period ended more than 6 months before the classification date, or a price that is not from the latest trading day the product holds. (evidence-statement side)
- [AC-15] A stock retired from acquisition MUST NOT be classified or counted in any peer set, and MUST be shown in its universes as not classified because it is retired. (display side: Compare and evidence render it as not followed)
- [AC-16] The user MUST be able to sort stocks by any factor's score or percentile, filter them with thresholds on one or more factors at once, and view chosen stocks side by side, both across all classified stocks and within a single universe. (compare side)
- [AC-17] For any classification, the user MUST be able to see the inputs it was built from and the date on which each input was known.
- [AC-18] Every classification MUST be retained with the date it was produced and MUST NOT be overwritten, and the user MUST be able to see a stock's earlier classifications.
- [AC-19] Data acquired for an earlier period MUST NOT change any classification already recorded, and MUST NOT make a classification appear to have been known before the date it was produced. (display side: history renders as recorded, nothing recomputed)
- [AC-23] A value or growth classification MUST be marked as possibly reflecting restated data when any fundamental behind it was acquired as historical backfill, for a period already reported before acquisition of that stock began, and the mark MUST be retained with that classification in its history. (display side)

### Derived

- [DAC-E5-1] Compare MUST hold 2 to 6 stocks; a stock not classified on a factor MUST keep its column with its reason rather than being dropped, and an unrecognised requested symbol MUST be reported, not silently absent. (traces to: AC-16)
- [DAC-E5-2] Stock evidence MUST show every input with its value (where present), `as_of` and `known_on`, and MUST name the specific missing or outdated input behind an incomplete factor with the recovery path when one exists. (traces to: AC-17, AC-13)
- [DAC-E5-3] A stock no longer followed MUST render "Not followed" on every factor in Compare and evidence, keeping its earlier classifications visible rather than disappearing. (traces to: AC-15)
- [DAC-E5-4] The Earlier scores / history tab MUST show only recorded results, newest first, and MUST be visibly distinct from "never scored." (traces to: AC-18, AC-19)
- [DAC-E5-5] The restatement mark MUST render on every percentile it applies to across Compare and Stock evidence, with the meaning sentence stated once per screen. (traces to: AC-23)
