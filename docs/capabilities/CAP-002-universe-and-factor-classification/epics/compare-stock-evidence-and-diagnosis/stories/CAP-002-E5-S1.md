# Comparison Table Pattern and Compare Screen

## User Story

As an owner-operator, I want 2 to 6 stocks side by side, none dropped for being incomplete, so that I can compare candidates on equal terms and see why one is not scored.

## Description

Delivers `web/src/components/patterns/comparison-table.tsx` (native `<table>` with programmatic row and column headers) and `web/src/screens/research/compare/index.tsx`. A stock incomplete or unranked on a factor keeps its column with its reason; a retired stock reads Not followed; per-input status follows DEC-0077; the per-input dates disclosure is keyboard operable. This is the `CompareScreen` the shell composition story routes to.

Source: `component-design-classification-ui.md` sections 2.5, 10, 7; `ux-design.md` section 1.5.

## Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)

### From Capability / EPIC

- [AC-15] A stock retired from acquisition MUST NOT be classified or counted in any peer set, and MUST be shown in its universes as not classified because it is retired. (display side: Compare and evidence render it as not followed)
- [AC-16] The user MUST be able to sort stocks by any factor's score or percentile, filter them with thresholds on one or more factors at once, and view chosen stocks side by side, both across all classified stocks and within a single universe. (compare side)
- [AC-23] A value or growth classification MUST be marked as possibly reflecting restated data when any fundamental behind it was acquired as historical backfill, for a period already reported before acquisition of that stock began, and the mark MUST be retained with that classification in its history. (display side)
- [DAC-E5-1] Compare MUST hold 2 to 6 stocks; a stock not classified on a factor MUST keep its column with its reason rather than being dropped, and an unrecognised requested symbol MUST be reported, not silently absent. (traces to: AC-16)
- [DAC-E5-3] A stock no longer followed MUST render "Not followed" on every factor in Compare and evidence, keeping its earlier classifications visible rather than disappearing. (traces to: AC-15)
- [DAC-E5-5] The restatement mark MUST render on every percentile it applies to across Compare and Stock evidence, with the meaning sentence stated once per screen. (traces to: AC-23)

### Derived

- [DAC-E5-S1-1] Given a stock incomplete on a factor, When compared, Then its column remains with Incomplete and the input named; not enough to rank is stated once per group. (traces to: DAC-E5-1)
- [DAC-E5-S1-2] Given six columns, When Add is attempted, Then it is `blocked` with A comparison holds up to 6 stocks.; given fewer than two, Then Choose 2 to 6 stocks to compare. is shown. (traces to: DAC-E5-1)
- [DAC-E5-S1-3] Given `instrument_not_found`, When an added symbol is unknown, Then it is a field error and no column is added; `unknown_symbols` from the server are reported. (traces to: DAC-E5-1)
- [DAC-E5-S1-4] Given a retired stock, When compared, Then every factor reads Not followed. (traces to: DAC-E5-3)
- [DAC-E5-S1-5] Given a restated value or growth percentile, When rendered, Then the mark appears on it with the meaning sentence once per screen. (traces to: DAC-E5-5)
