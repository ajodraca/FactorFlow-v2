# Volume Test: 3000 Followed Stocks and the 2-Second p95 on GET /api/data-health

## User Story
As an owner-operator, I want Data health proven to answer within two seconds at the roster's upper bound, so that the one expensive read never makes the screen wait.

## Description
Writes `tests/integration/test_data_health_volume.py` (data-health-reader 2.1, 7; data-health-api 5): seeds 3000 active instruments with failures on 300, asserts the counts, the keyset pages and bounded memory, then times 20 `GET /api/data-health` requests through the in-process application after one warm-up request and asserts a 95th percentile of at most 2 seconds (AC-28; system design constraint 7; capability assumption 4). The quantitative bounds are DAC-E2-14's.

Source: component-design-data-health-reader.md 5, 7; component-design-data-health-api.md 5; epic CAP-003-E2 NFR *Latency*, DAC-E2-14.

## Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)

### From Capability / EPIC
- [AC-28] With 3,000 followed stocks, `GET /api/data-health` MUST answer within 2 seconds at the 95th percentile, measured by the volume test.
- [DAC-E2-14] The volume test MUST seed 3000 active instruments with failures on 300, assert the counts, the keyset pages and bounded memory, and time `GET /api/data-health` through the in-process application to a 95th percentile of at most 2 seconds after one warm-up request. (traces to: AC-28)

### Derived
- [DAC-E2-S7-1] Given the seeded store, When `GET /api/attention` is requested, Then `problems.stock_count` is 300 and `followed_count` 3000, and `GET /api/data-health`'s `affected_count` equals 300 with six `read_coverage` pages consumed. (traces to: AC-16)
- [DAC-E2-S7-2] Given the timing loop, When it reports, Then the p95 is computed over the 20 timed requests only, the warm-up excluded, and a failure names the measured p95 in the assertion message. (traces to: DAC-E2-14)
