# Vocabulary Sweep and the Delivered Journeys at the New Paths

## User Story

As an owner-operator, I want proof that no domain term survives on the five taken-over screens and that every journey CAP-001 accepted still works at the new paths, so that the takeover changed the words and places and nothing else.

## Description

Logical component: `owner-web-app`. Creates `web/src/screens/vocabulary.test.tsx`, the AC-19 sweep of `component-design-owner-web-app.md` §7: renders each of the five screens in its populated, empty, error and dialog states and asserts none of *instrument*, *acquisition*, *acquire*, *run*, *runs*, *Retire*, *Retired*, *Provider credential* appears as a whole word, in any case, in visible text or accessible names. Updates the eleven delivered e2e specs (`first-run`, `diagnose-failure`, `cancel-run`, `cancel-in-progress`, `coverage-empty`, `coverage-expansion`, `instrument-changes`, `instruments-add-retire`, `run-detail`, `runs-start-cancel`, `unrecognised-instrument`) and `tests/e2e/support/acquisition.ts` so `page.goto` and label locators follow the new paths and labels while the behaviour each proves is unchanged (§2.6). `web/src/api/client.*.test.ts` and `render.test.tsx` stay unchanged.

Breakdown and ordering: DEC-0223.

## Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)

### From Capability / EPIC

- [AC-17] Every action, confirmation and refusal CAP-001 accepted MUST remain available through this shell with the same outcome at the new paths.
- [AC-19] Every user-visible label on the five screens MUST use the display label STD-UI-1 defines and MUST NOT show the domain term beside it.
- [DAC-E7-3] A vocabulary sweep test MUST render each of the five screens in its populated, empty, error and dialog states and assert none of *instrument*, *acquisition*, *acquire*, *run*, *runs*, *Retire*, *Retired*, *Provider credential* appears as a whole word, in any case, in visible text or accessible names. (traces to: AC-19)
- [DAC-E7-12] The delivered screen tests, the eleven e2e specs and `tests/e2e/support/acquisition.ts` MUST follow the new paths and labels as owner-web-app §2.6 lists, with `web/src/api/client.*.test.ts` and `render.test.tsx` unchanged. (traces to: AC-17)

### Derived

- [DAC-E7-S7-1] Given the sweep over twenty renders (five screens, four states), When it runs, Then it passes, and a deliberate fixture containing *Retired* makes it fail with the screen, state and term named. (traces to: DAC-E7-3)
- [DAC-E7-S7-2] Given the sweep's word list, When *Coverage*, *gap*, *history depth*, *trading day*, *Covered from*, *Covered to*, *Depth*, *Reference*, *Prices*, *Fundamentals* and *Corporate actions* appear, Then they do not fail it. (traces to: DAC-E7-3)

- [DAC-E7-S7-4] Given the eleven specs run against a real stack, When each completes, Then each proves the outcome it proved for CAP-001 at the new path, with locators by the new labels. (traces to: AC-17)
- [DAC-E7-S7-5] Given `git diff` of `web/src/api/client.coverage.test.ts`, `client.runs.test.ts` and `render.test.tsx`, When inspected, Then it is empty. (traces to: DAC-E7-12)
