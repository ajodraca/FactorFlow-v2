# Tasks: CAP-003-E7-S2 — Coverage Under the Shell: Display Labels, the Virtualised Table, ?symbol= Seeding and Links to the New Paths

**Story**: `repos/factorflow-v2/FactorFlow-v2/docs/capabilities/CAP-003-app-shell-and-data-health/epics/taking-over-the-delivered-screens/stories/CAP-003-E7-S2.md`

## Summary

Takes over CAP-001's Coverage screen (`web/src/screens/coverage/*`) at `/data/coverage` (owner-web-app 2.1, 2.3, 2.4; UX design 1.5). The screen keeps every request, filter, refusal and cadence CAP-001 accepted and changes only its words, its components and its entry: *instrument* becomes *stock* throughout (*Stock* column, *No stock matches this filter.*, *No data has been downloaded yet.*), *Last acquired* becomes *Last downloaded*, *Retired* becomes *Stopped following*, and the kept domain terms (*Coverage*, *gap*, *Covered from*, *Covered to*, *Depth*, *Reference*, *Prices*, *Fundamentals*, *Corporate actions*) stay. The table moves onto CAP-003-E1-S4's `data-table` `virtualised` variant with its per-row expansion, sitting on a `card`; the delivered Previous / Next page buttons, which replaced one keyset page with the next, give way to keyset pages appended as the window nears the loaded end (DAC-E7-S2-2). `?symbol={SYMBOL}` seeds the symbol filter on load and editing or clearing the filter rewrites the query with `replace` (DAC-E7-6). Presence, the depth shortfall and the gap-detection *Unknown* render through `StatusBadge` and `NotDeterminable` (DAC-E7-S2-5), the screen's states through `StateRegion` (DAC-E7-S2-6), and times through `formatInstant`. **Download now**, its two blocked reasons, the queued sentence and the route to `/data/downloads/{run_id}` are rendered by the shared `StartRunControl` that CAP-003-E7-S4 rewrites, and the *Stopped following* marker by the `LifecycleBadge` CAP-003-E7-S3 rewrites; this story composes both and proves them on Coverage, so both are hard dependencies added to the graph. The story writes `entry-hint.test.tsx` and adds `web/src/screens/coverage/` to the AC-26 literal scan.
