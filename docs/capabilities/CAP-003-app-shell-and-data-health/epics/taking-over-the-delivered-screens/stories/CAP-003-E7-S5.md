# Download Detail Under the Shell: the Crumb, Determinate Progress, the Two Cancel Confirmations and the Not Found Diagnosis

## User Story
As an owner-operator, I want a download's detail at `/data/downloads/:id` titled by when it started, with its progress and a plain diagnosis of a symbol the provider does not recognise, so that I know what to do without being told to download again.

## Description
Takes over `web/src/screens/runs/detail/*` and the detail tests in `web/src/screens/runs/` (owner-web-app 2.1, 2.3): `h1` *Download {start time}*; the top-bar crumb set through `useShell().setTitleDetail(formatDownloadName(...))` once the run is read and cleared on unmount (DEC-0214); **Cancel run** becomes **Cancel download**; per-stock reason lines say *downloading*; progress while running is `progress` `determinate` *{done} of {total} stocks* polled at v5's 10 seconds with its last-updated time and no estimated time remaining, `indeterminate` only while the total is unknown; the two cancel confirmations keep their wording and outcomes (*Stop download* then *Stopping*; *Cancel download* straight to *Cancelled by you*) on `dialog` `confirm`; the already-finished and interrupted sentences; a *Not found* stock renders `status-warning` *Not found* with *The provider doesn't recognise this symbol. Downloading again won't help; stop following it or follow a different symbol.* and **Open Data health** to `/data`; the roster becomes `data-table` `expandable`. Replaces every caller of `detail/result-outcome-badge.tsx` with `StatusBadge` and deletes it with its test (DAC-E7-5). The completion toast is the shell's and is suppressed on this route (E3-S7). `/data` is registered by E6-S7; the link's target is asserted as a path here.

Source: component-design-owner-web-app.md 2.1, 2.3, 2.6, 7; UX design 1.8; epic CAP-003-E7 DAC-E7-5, DAC-E7-8, DAC-E7-9, DAC-E7-11.
