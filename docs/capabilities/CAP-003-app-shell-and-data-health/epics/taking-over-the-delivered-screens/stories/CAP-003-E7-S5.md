# Download Detail at `/data/downloads/:id`: Crumb, Progress, Confirmations and the *Not Found* Diagnosis

## User Story

As an owner-operator, I want a download's detail named by its start time in the top bar, showing determinate progress while it runs, keeping the two cancel confirmations, and diagnosing an unrecognised symbol with where to fix it, so that I can follow and stop a download and understand a failure without a raw identifier anywhere.

## Description

Logical component: `owner-web-app`. Takes over `web/src/screens/runs/detail/*` as `component-design-owner-web-app.md` §2.3 specifies: the `h1` *Download {start time}* and the top-bar crumb set through `useShell().setTitleDetail(formatDownloadName(...))` once the run is read and cleared on unmount (DEC-0214); **Cancel run** becomes **Cancel download** (`button` `secondary`); per-stock reason lines say *downloading*; progress while running is E1-S8's `progress` `determinate` *{done} of {total} stocks* polled at 10 seconds with its last-updated time and no estimated time remaining (`indeterminate` only while the total is unknown); the two cancel confirmations on `dialog` `confirm` with their wording (*Stop download* then *Stopping*; **Cancel download** straight to *Cancelled by you*); the already-finished and interrupted sentences; a *Not found* stock with *The provider doesn't recognise this symbol. Downloading again won't help; stop following it or follow a different symbol.* and **Open Data health** to `/data`; the summary on `card` `summary`; the roster on `data-table` `expandable`; outcomes through `StatusBadge`. Deletes `web/src/screens/runs/outcome-badge.tsx` and `detail/result-outcome-badge.tsx` with their tests once no caller remains (E7-S4 switched the list; E3-S5 deleted the status region). The completion toast is the shell's and is suppressed on this route (E3-S6).

Breakdown and ordering: DEC-0223.
