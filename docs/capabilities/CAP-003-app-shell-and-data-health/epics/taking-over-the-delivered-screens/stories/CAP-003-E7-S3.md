# Followed Stocks at `/data/followed`: Words, Confirm Dialog and the `focusAdd` Entry Hint

## User Story

As an owner-operator, I want the followed-stocks screen to speak of following and stopping following, confirm stopping with the product's wording and keep history, and focus its add field when I arrive to replace a symbol, so that managing what I follow reads like the rest of the product.

## Description

Logical component: `owner-web-app`. Takes over `web/src/screens/instruments/*` as `component-design-owner-web-app.md` §2.3 and §2.4 specify: labels per UX design 1.6 (**Follow a stock**, **Follow many**, **Stop following**, *Stopped following*, *followed*, *No stocks followed yet.*, the add field named *Symbol of the stock to follow*); the retire dialog rebuilt on E1-S7's `dialog` `confirm` with *Stop following {SYMBOL}? Its history is kept, and strategies holding it keep holding it. You can follow it again later.* and **Stop following**, plus v5's conditional sentences relabelled; a stock the provider does not recognise carrying `badge` `status-warning` *Not found* through `StatusBadge`; the table on E1-S9's `data-table` `virtualised`; the add field on `form` `inline` and `input` `text`; **Follow many** on `textarea` `list` keeping `parse-bulk-symbols.ts` unchanged; `location.state.focusAdd` focusing the add field on mount; Coverage links reading `/data/coverage?symbol={SYMBOL}`. The delivered `instruments/*.test.tsx` follow (§2.6) and `entry-hint.test.tsx` is new. The directory keeps its delivered name.

Breakdown and ordering: DEC-0223.
