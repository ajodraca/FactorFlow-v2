# Followed Stocks Under the Shell: Follow, Follow Many, Stop Following with dialog confirm, Not Found and the Focused Add Field

## User Story
As an owner-operator, I want the followed-stocks screen at `/data/followed` in the product's words, with the add field ready when Data health sends me to follow a different symbol, so that fixing a typo is one keystroke away.

## Description
Takes over `web/src/screens/instruments/*` (owner-web-app 2.1, 2.3, 2.4; the directory keeps its delivered name, DAC-E7-13): **Add instrument** becomes **Follow a stock**, **Add many** becomes **Follow many** (keeping the delivered `parse-bulk-symbols.ts` unchanged, which setup imports), **Retire** becomes **Stop following**, *Retired* becomes *Stopped following*, *active* becomes *followed*, *No instruments named yet.* becomes *No stocks followed yet.*, the add field is named *Symbol of the stock to follow*; the confirmation is `dialog` `confirm` with the blueprint §9 wording and **Stop following**, plus v5's conditional sentences relabelled; a stock the provider does not recognise carries `badge` `status-warning` *Not found* through `StatusBadge`; opened with `location.state.focusAdd` the add field takes focus on mount and a reload does not re-focus; Coverage links read `/data/coverage?symbol={SYMBOL}`; the table becomes `data-table` `virtualised`. Writes `entry-hint.test.tsx`; `retire-dialog.test.tsx` and the other delivered tests follow (owner-web-app 2.6).

Source: component-design-owner-web-app.md 2.1, 2.3, 2.4, 2.6, 7; UX design 1.6; epic CAP-003-E7 DAC-E7-1, DAC-E7-4, DAC-E7-6, DAC-E7-9, DAC-E7-11, DAC-E7-13.
