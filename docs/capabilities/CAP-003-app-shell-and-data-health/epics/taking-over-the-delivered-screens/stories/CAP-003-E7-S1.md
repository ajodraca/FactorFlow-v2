# Settings Under the Shell: Market Data Key, Open Setup and the Relabelled Depth Form

## User Story
As an owner-operator, I want Settings at `/settings` in the product's words with a way back into setup, so that I can check the key, the depth and the followed list without hunting.

## Description
Takes over `web/src/screens/settings/*` (owner-web-app 2.1, 2.3): under the `h1` *Settings*, *Walk through setup again to check the market data key, history depth and followed stocks.* with **Open setup** (a link to `/setup`, `button` `outline`); the *Provider credential* region becomes *Market data key* with *Found* or *Missing* through `StatusBadge`, never the value, the variable and restart sentence when missing, and `coverage-unknown` *Not determinable* while health is unreachable, never *Missing*; the history depth form rebuilt on `form` and `input` `numeric` with its behaviour unchanged. The delivered `credential-status.test.tsx`, `credential-badge.test.tsx` and `history-depth-form.test.tsx` follow the labels and components (owner-web-app 2.6). The screen is already mounted at `/settings` by E3-S4; `/setup` is registered by E4-S6, so the link's target is asserted as a path here.

Source: component-design-owner-web-app.md 2.1, 2.3, 2.5, 2.6, 4, 7; UX design 1.9; epic CAP-003-E7 DAC-E7-7.
