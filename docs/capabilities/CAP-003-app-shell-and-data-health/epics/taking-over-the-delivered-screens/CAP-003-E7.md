# Taking Over the Delivered Screens

**EPIC ID:** CAP-003-E7
**Capability:** CAP-003
**Summary:** Move the five screens CAP-001 delivered, Coverage, Instruments, Runs, Run detail and Settings, under the product shell at `/data/coverage`, `/data/followed`, `/data/downloads`, `/data/downloads/:id` and `/settings`, in the product's own words and built from the design system, without changing what they do. Every user-visible string takes the STD-UI-1 v6 display label (stock, followed stock, download, stop following, Complete, Partial, Failed, Cancelled by you, Interrupted) and never shows the domain term beside it; every status renders through the one tier function with its label and shape; the hand-built elements are replaced by the vendored components; every internal link and navigation follows the new paths, including the three Research link targets CAP-002's design assigns to this capability; and the delivered unit and end-to-end tests follow the labels and paths with the behaviour they prove unchanged. Two entry hints (a seeded coverage filter, a focused add field) and Settings' **Open setup** link are the only additions, and none changes an action's outcome. CAP-001's approved design is not reopened: this capability owns the change (AGT-SCOPE-002; DEC-0211).
**Actors:** Owner-operator; `owner-web-app`; `owner-shell` (routes, `setTitleDetail`); `ui-components`; `ingestion-api` (every delivered endpoint, unchanged); `classification-ui`'s `copy.ts` (the one `SHELL_ROUTES` edit).
**Estimate:** L
**Out of Scope:** Any change to a request, query parameter, confirmation step, refusal or cadence CAP-001 accepted (system design constraint 2); the route map, titles and legacy redirects (E3); the vendored components (E1); any CAP-002 screen beyond the three `SHELL_ROUTES` targets (AC-27); removing the legacy redirects; a stored or derived download number (capability OQ-1, answered no); the delivered `parse-bulk-symbols.ts`, imported by setup and kept by **Follow many** unchanged.
**Dependencies:** E1 (`data-table` `virtualised`, `history` and `expandable`, `dialog` `confirm`, `select`, `badge`, `button`, `form`, `input`, `textarea`, `progress`, `card`, `StatusBadge`, `StateRegion`, formatters) and E3 (the routes at the new paths and the top-bar crumb mechanism); E6 and E4 reach these screens by link (`?symbol=`, `focusAdd`, **Open setup**) and E5 by **See download** and **Open Settings**. Cross-capability: CAP-001 (`requires-runtime`): every endpoint is delivered and consumed unchanged; CAP-001's acceptance criteria for these screens (AC-1, AC-2, AC-4, AC-7 to AC-11, AC-13, AC-17, AC-19 to AC-26) are the contract the relabelled tests keep proving. CAP-002 (`requires-runtime`): the owner fixed the order, CAP-002 lands first (capability OQ-2), so the `SHELL_ROUTES` edit in `web/src/screens/research/copy.ts` is sequenced after `classification-ui` has merged; nothing else in this EPIC waits on it. STD-UI-1, STD-UI-2, STD-UI-12 and the blueprint §7, §8, §9 (`conforms-to`) are designed against.
**Links:** system-design.md §3.5 (boxes 13 to 15, note 3), §4.1 *Existing components this design changes*, §4.2 (`Screen registration` for `owner-web-app`, the `ingestion-api` rows), constraints 2, 3, 4, 15; component-design-owner-web-app.md §2.1 to §2.6, §3, §7, §8, §10; ux-design.md §1.5 to §1.9, §0.7; CAP-001 `component-design-owner-web-app.md` v4 and CAP-001 `ux-design.md` v5 §1.1 to §1.5 (the screens as delivered, not edited).

## Goals

- Serve the five screens at their new paths inside the shell with every internal link, `navigate()` call and e2e `page.goto` following, so nothing in the product depends on the legacy redirects.
- Relabel every user-visible string on the five screens to the display vocabulary and prove it with a vocabulary sweep test over every state of every screen.
- Render every download state, result outcome and stock state through `StatusBadge`, deleting the delivered `outcome-badge.tsx` and `result-outcome-badge.tsx` once no caller remains.
- Rebuild each screen's controls from `ui-components`: virtualised tables with row expansion over the delivered keyset pages, `dialog` `confirm` for stopping following and cancelling, `select` filters, `form` and `input` for depth and the add field, `progress` on the download detail.
- Add the two entry hints and Settings' **Open setup** link, the *Market data key* region and the download detail's crumb and determinate progress, changing no action's outcome.
- Migrate the three `SHELL_ROUTES` targets in CAP-002's `copy.ts` to the new paths and nothing else in that file.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **Behaviour preserved** (AC-17; system design constraint 2): no request, query parameter, confirmation step, refusal or cadence changes; the delivered screen tests and the eleven e2e specs change only where a label, path or component changes and keep proving the same outcomes.
- **Volume** (CAP-001 AC-4; STD-UI-30): keyset pages with windowed rendering at 3000 stocks, now inside the `virtualised` `data-table` variant with no virtualisation library added (DEC-0215); the Downloads and detail polls stay at 10 seconds while non-terminal.
- **Accessibility** (AC-25; STD-UI-24): CAP-001 v5's semantics unchanged with the display labels in every accessible name: programmatic row and column headers and `aria-sort`; every presence state carrying its word; the expander named for its row; filtering that does not move focus and announces the count politely; the row action *Stop following AAPL*; the start-time link named *Download started {time}, open details*; dialog focus trapped and returned to the triggering control, now supplied by Radix; polled regions announcing politely.
- **Security** (AC-4): no credential field anywhere, presence only, `coverage-unknown` rather than *Missing* while health is unreachable; no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`; no storage; same origin.
- **Layout** (AC-26): the four table screens at full width and Settings at `--measure-prose` inside the shell's main region, each table on a `card`; v5's tokens unchanged.
- **Operations:** no data migration; rollback is redeploying the previous image, which restores the old paths and words together.

## EPIC Acceptance Criteria

### From Capability

- [AC-4] Settings MUST state whether the market data credential was found and, when it is missing, which environment variable to set, and MUST NOT display its value.
- [AC-5] **Follow many** on the followed-stocks screen MUST keep accepting the valid entries and naming each rejected one, through the same parser setup uses.
- [AC-12] The downloads list and the download detail MUST never present a partial or interrupted download as complete.
- [AC-14] The download detail MUST diagnose a stock the provider does not recognise as *Not found* and point to Data health, never suggesting that downloading again would help.
- [AC-15] **Download now** on Coverage and Downloads MUST start a full download and MUST say when it starts after a running one.
- [AC-17] Every action, confirmation and refusal CAP-001 accepted on its coverage, instrument, run, run-detail and settings screens MUST remain available through this shell with the same outcome, at `/data/coverage`, `/data/followed`, `/data/downloads`, `/data/downloads/:id` and `/settings`.
- [AC-18] Every internal link on the five screens and the three Research link targets MUST point at the new paths directly, so none depends on the legacy redirects and none breaks when they are removed.
- [AC-19] Every user-visible label on the five screens MUST use the display label STD-UI-1 defines and MUST NOT show the domain term beside it.
- [AC-20] Every download outcome, result outcome and stock state on the five screens MUST render as the single severity tier the blueprint's status map assigns it, with its label and its tier shape, never colour alone.
- [AC-24] Each screen MUST keep its eight-state table, relabelled, rendered through `StateRegion`.
- [AC-26] The five screens MUST be built from `ui-components` and consume theme tokens only.
- [AC-27] The `SHELL_ROUTES` edit MUST leave every CAP-002 route and screen otherwise untouched.
