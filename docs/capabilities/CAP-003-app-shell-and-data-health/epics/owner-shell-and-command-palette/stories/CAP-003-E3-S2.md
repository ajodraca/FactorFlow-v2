# Polled Reads, the Health Provider and the Shell Context

## User Story

As an owner-operator, I want the three shell-wide reads held in one context at recorded cadences and paused while the page is hidden, so that every screen shows the same facts, no screen opens an interval of its own, and nothing polls a tab I am not looking at.

## Description

Logical component: `owner-shell`. Creates `web/src/app/use-polled-read.ts` (`usePolledRead(read, intervalMs)`), `web/src/app/health.tsx` (`HealthProvider`, `useHealth`, `formatTimestamp` and `UNREACHABLE_MESSAGE` moved from the delivered `status-region.tsx`, plus `lastAnsweredAt` and the visibility pause, DEC-0212 PROVISIONAL), `web/src/app/shell-context.tsx` (`ShellProvider`, `useShell()` with `health`, `attention`, `scores`, `refresh(kind)`, `setTitleDetail`) and `web/src/app/use-data-health.ts` (`useDataHealth()`, the on-demand aggregate hook for `data-health-ui` alone, DEC-0219 withdrawn), as `component-design-owner-shell.md` §2.3 and §2.4 specify. Cadences: health 30 s; attention and summary 60 s, 10 s while `latest_download.state` is `requested`, `running` or `cancelling` or the summary's `later.state` is `building`; a summary `404` is `not_served` and re-read on the 60 s cadence.

To keep the delivered shell passing until E3-S5 deletes the status region, `status-region.tsx` is edited in this story only to import `HealthProvider`, `useHealth`, `formatTimestamp` and `UNREACHABLE_MESSAGE` from `health.tsx` instead of defining them; its polling, unknown-state and cleanup cases move to `health.test.tsx` now and `status-region.test.tsx` keeps the rendering cases until E3-S5 removes it.

Breakdown and ordering: DEC-0223.

## Technical Details

### API Contract

Consumes `GET /api/health` (delivered, 30 s), `GET /api/attention` and `GET /api/classifications/summary` (60 s or 10 s), `GET /api/data-health` (on demand), all through E3-S1's fetch module and the delivered `fetchHealth`. Exposes `useShell(): ShellContextValue` and `useDataHealth(): { state: ReadState<DataHealth>; retry(): void }`.
