# Route Map, Destinations, Titles, Legacy Redirects and Not Found: the Five Delivered Screens at Their New Paths

## User Story
As an owner-operator, I want the product to open on setup or Home from the health answer, my old bookmarks to arrive at the new paths, and the five delivered screens titled at `/data/*` and `/settings`, so that nothing I reach today becomes unreachable.

## Description
Rewrites `web/src/app/routes.tsx` as owner-shell 2.6's table for what exists at this point: `/` as `RootRedirect` (one card-shaped skeleton until the first health answer, then `Navigate replace` to `/setup` when `health.most_recent_run` is null and to `/home` otherwise; DEC-0202), the five delivered screens at `/data/coverage`, `/data/followed`, `/data/downloads`, `/data/downloads/:id` and `/settings` (they exist in `web/src/screens/coverage`, `instruments`, `runs`, `runs/detail`, `settings`, so E3 registers them itself under DEC-0223 answer A; E7 relabels them later), the four legacy redirects and `*` as `NotFound`; CAP-002's five `Route` elements are kept unchanged if `classification-ui` has merged (AC-27; DEC-0204). Creates `destinations.ts` (`DESTINATIONS`, static and ordered, holding at this point only `{ group: "data", label: "Settings", to: "/settings" }` and, when CAP-002 has merged, the Research entry after the Home slot, with `RESEARCH_REGISTERED` exported beside it; Home and Data health enter in the E5 and E6 closing stories that register their screens, per DAC-E3-2), `titles.ts` (the route-to-title table with crumbs and the `setTitleDetail` generic noun; DEC-0214), `legacy-redirects.tsx` (owner-shell 2.6 code, query kept, one module deleted one release later) and `not-found.tsx`. Rewrites `routes.test.tsx` and writes `titles.test.tsx` and `legacy-redirects.test.tsx`. `/setup`, `/home` and `/data` are registered by CAP-003-E4-S6, E5-S5 and E6-S7.

Source: component-design-owner-shell.md 2.5, 2.6, 2.8, 2.10, 7; epic CAP-003-E3 DAC-E3-1 to DAC-E3-4, DAC-E3-9; DEC-0223.

## Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)

### From Capability / EPIC
- [AC-2] Until the first download has been started, the product MUST open on setup; afterwards on Home.
- [AC-17] Coverage, followed stocks, downloads, download detail and settings MUST open in the same shell, titled in the top bar, at their new paths.
- [AC-18] Each path CAP-001 shipped MUST take the owner to the same screen at its new path for one release.
- [AC-27] CAP-002's routes MUST stay unchanged and reachable.
- [DAC-E3-1] `/` MUST render one skeleton until the first health answer, then `Navigate replace` to `/setup` or `/home`; setup incomplete is not a lock. (traces to: AC-2, AC-21)
- [DAC-E3-2] `DESTINATIONS` MUST be one static ordered list; a destination enters it only in the change that registers its screen; `RESEARCH_REGISTERED` MUST be true exactly when the Research entry is present. (traces to: AC-1, AC-27)
- [DAC-E3-3] The four legacy paths MUST land on their new paths by `Navigate replace` with the search string kept, from one module. (traces to: AC-18)
- [DAC-E3-4] An unregistered path MUST render the not-found state inside the frame, titled *Page not found*, with *There's no page at this address.* and a **Home** link to `/home`. (traces to: AC-24)
- [DAC-E3-9] Top-bar titles MUST come from a static route-to-title table with each ancestor crumb a link; a detail crumb reads the generic noun until set. (traces to: AC-17)

### Derived
- [DAC-E3-S4-1] Given `/runs/abc?x=1`, When visited, Then the location becomes `/data/downloads/abc?x=1` with `replace`; the same for `/coverage`, `/instruments` and `/runs` to `/data/coverage`, `/data/followed` and `/data/downloads`. (traces to: DAC-E3-3)
- [DAC-E3-S4-2] Given `titles.ts`, When every registered route is enumerated, Then each has a title: *Settings*, *Data health › Coverage*, *Data health › Followed stocks*, *Data health › Downloads*, *Data health › Downloads › Download* (generic noun until set), *Page not found*, and the Research titles when registered; the *Data health* crumb links to `/data` and *Downloads* to `/data/downloads` (owner-shell 2.6). - [DAC-E3-S4-3] Given `routes.test.tsx`, When it runs, Then it asserts only the routes this story registers: it visits the five delivered screens at their new paths, asserts the not-found state for an unknown path and the `/` redirect targets by URL, and asserts nothing about `/setup`, `/home` or `/data` content, which later stories register (backlog review v2 FINDING-2). (traces to: AC-17)
