# Route Map, Destinations, Titles, Legacy and Root Redirects and the Not-Found State

## User Story

As an owner-operator, I want the product to open on setup or Home from `/`, keep my old bookmarks working at the new paths, and tell me when a page does not exist, so that the five delivered screens are reachable at their product paths before any new screen lands.

## Description

Logical component: `owner-shell`. Rewrites `web/src/app/routes.tsx` to the route table of `component-design-owner-shell.md` §2.6 and creates `web/src/app/destinations.ts` (`DESTINATIONS`, `RESEARCH_REGISTERED`; DEC-0204 PROVISIONAL), `web/src/app/titles.ts` (the route-to-title table; DEC-0214 PROVISIONAL), `web/src/app/legacy-redirects.tsx` (the four `Navigate replace` routes with the search string kept, §2.6 verbatim) and `web/src/app/not-found.tsx`. `RootRedirect` renders one card-shaped skeleton until the first health answer, then `/setup` when `health.most_recent_run` is null and `/home` otherwise (DEC-0202).

Registration in this story: the five delivered screens at `/data/coverage`, `/data/followed`, `/data/downloads`, `/data/downloads/:id` and `/settings` with their titles, and the Settings destination, because those screens already exist in `web/src/screens/` (E7 relabels them in place). `/setup`, `/home` and `/data` and the Home and Data health destinations enter in E3-S7, E3-S8 and E3-S9, each in the change that registers its screen (DAC-E3-2; DEC-0223). The Research destination and `classification-ui`'s five `Route` elements are carried, unchanged, if `web/src/screens/research/` exists in the clone when this story is implemented (CAP-002 lands first, capability OQ-2); otherwise `RESEARCH_REGISTERED` is false and CAP-002 adds them here (E3 open question 4; DEC-0204). Until E3-S5 rewrites `shell.tsx`, the delivered shell renders this route map.

Breakdown and ordering: DEC-0223.
