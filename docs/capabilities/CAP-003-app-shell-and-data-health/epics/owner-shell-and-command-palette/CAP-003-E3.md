# Owner Shell and Command Palette

**EPIC ID:** CAP-003-E3
**Capability:** CAP-003
**Summary:** Replace the shell CAP-001 built for one capability with the product-wide owner interface shell: a rail in two groups listing only shipped destinations with their attention counts, a sticky top bar carrying the route's title or breadcrumb and the palette opener, the route map with the `/` redirect to setup or Home and the four legacy redirects that keep old bookmarks working for one release, the designed unreachable and not-found states, and the three shell-wide polled reads (health, attention, classification summary) shared with every screen through one React context at recorded cadences. The command palette, opened from `⌘K` or `Ctrl+K` and the top bar on every route, finds followed stocks, universes and pages and only ever navigates. CAP-001's status region is retired, its two facts moving to the unreachable state and to Home and Data health, and CAP-002's interim Research rail entry is replaced with its routes untouched. The palette sits in this EPIC because the shell owns its shortcut and opener and it carries one criterion (DEC-0222).
**Actors:** Owner-operator; `owner-shell`; `command-palette`; `ingestion-api` (`GET /api/health`, `GET /api/instruments`); `data-health-api` (`GET /api/attention`, `GET /api/data-health`, `GET /api/data-health/problems`); `classification-api` (`GET /api/classifications/summary`, `GET /api/universes`); the screens it mounts, `setup-ui`, `home-ui`, `data-health-ui`, `owner-web-app`'s five and `classification-ui`'s five.
**Estimate:** L
**Out of Scope:** The screens themselves (E4 to E7) and their internal links (E7); the vendored components and patterns the shell imports (E1); any sign-in, user menu or account area (capability §5.3); the kill switch and the environment region, which ship with paper trading and for which no space is reserved (blueprint D-7); the Strategies and Portfolio destinations and a palette Actions group (absent, not disabled); removing the legacy redirects, due one release later; a dark theme switch; the universes read's shape, consumed as `classification-api` publishes it.
**Dependencies:** E1 (`ui-components`: `badge`, `button`, `card`, `toast`, `command`, `deriveNeedsYou`, `formatInstant`), and E2 for the attention read behind the count chips; the shell frame, route map, redirects, health context and states can be built against E1 alone, with the chips landing once E2 serves `/api/attention`. E4 to E7 register their screens into this EPIC's route map and `DESTINATIONS` as they land. Cross-capability: CAP-001 (`requires-runtime`) supplies `GET /api/health` and the delivered `HealthProvider`, `fetchHealth`, `requestJson`, `ApiError` and `ApiUnreachableError`, all delivered. CAP-002 (`requires-runtime`): the owner fixed the order, CAP-002 lands first (capability OQ-2), so the change that rewrites `shell.tsx` replaces CAP-002's appended rail entry with `{ group: "primary", label: "Research", to: "/research" }` and keeps its five `Route` elements unchanged (AC-27); `GET /api/classifications/summary` is consumed as published and answers `404` until deployed, which renders as not determinable rather than blocking anything. The blueprint and STD-UI (`conforms-to`) are designed against.
**Links:** system-design.md §2 blocks B1, B2, §3.1 (all steps and notes 1 to 5), §3.3 boxes 17 to 18, §3.5 (boxes 1 to 12 and notes 1 to 4), §4.1 `owner-shell`, `command-palette`, §4.2 (`Shell context`, `Screen registration` rows, `Palette dialog`, `GET /api/health`, `GET /api/classifications/summary`, `GET /api/universes`), constraints 8, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15; component-design-owner-shell.md §2.1 to §2.10, §3, §7, §8; component-design-command-palette.md §2.1 to §2.4, §3, §7, §8; ux-design.md §0.1 to §0.6, §1.1, §2.

## Goals

- Render every owner-interface route inside one frame (rail, top bar, main region) whose rail lists Home, Research (once its routes are registered), Data health and Settings in two groups and nothing else, with Strategies and Portfolio absent rather than disabled.
- Open `/` on setup until a download has been requested and on Home afterwards, from `GET /api/health`'s `most_recent_run`, after the first health answer and with a skeleton until then.
- Keep the four paths CAP-001 shipped reaching their screens by client-side `Navigate replace` to the new paths with the query kept, in one module deleted one release later.
- Hold the three shell-wide reads in one context at the recorded cadences (health 30 s; attention and summary 60 s, 10 s while a download is non-terminal or scores are building), paused while the document is hidden with an immediate read on show, with `refresh(kind)` for a screen to re-read after an action.
- Show the two attention counts as chips that are absent at zero, named in words, announced politely and never carrying a time of their own, the Home and Data health *checked {time}* lines standing for them (capability §5.4 STD-UI-25 specialization).
- Replace only the main region with the unreachable state when health stops answering, keeping the rail and top bar, showing *Last answered {time}*, and remounting the same URL without a reload when it answers again.
- Open the palette from the keyboard and the top bar on every route, with Stocks searched on the server by prefix, Universes when registered and Pages, every item navigating and none trading.
- Hold the approved-specification layout dimensions in one module, `web/src/app/layout.ts`, consumed by the shell and the screens.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **Cadences** (AC-25; STD-UI-25; system design constraint 8): health every 30 s (delivered); attention and summary every 60 s, every 10 s while `latest_download.state` is `requested`, `running` or `cancelling` or the summary's `later.state` is `building`; the summary's `404` is re-read on the 60 s cadence so a later deployment is picked up; no interval runs while the page is hidden; two reads of one kind never overlap. Three intervals for the whole interface, none per screen.
- **Performance:** `GET /api/attention` is bounded aggregates (E2); the summary read has no stock rows; route-level code splitting by `React.lazy` keeps the first load to the shell and the landing screen; the palette sends at most one request per settled keystroke (250 ms debounce) returning at most 8 rows, aborting an in-flight search when the text changes.
- **Accessibility** (AC-25; STD-UI-24): `nav` labelled *Main* with `aria-current="page"`; the top bar a `banner` whose first text is the title; one `main` with one `h1` per screen, focus moving to it on route change; the single `keydown` listener ignores `input`, `textarea`, `select` and `contenteditable`; the palette restores focus to its opener on close; toasts never take focus and `error` and `warning` ones persist until dismissed; contrast 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for boundaries, the chip pair checked against `design-system/theme-validation.md`.
- **Security** (capability §5.3; DEC-0208): loopback, no authentication, no sign-in or account area; `credential_present` passed through the context and never a value (AC-4); same-origin reads only; no browser storage; React escaping with no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`; typed palette text reaches the server only as the `symbol` query parameter the delivered route validates (SEC-INP-001); no dependency of its own beyond E1's.
- **Layout** (AC-26; blueprint §3.1): design width 1280 px and above; below 768 px the rail stacks above the content and nothing becomes unreachable; the 208 px rail and 1180 px cap read from `layout.ts`.
- **Operations:** no data migration; rollback is redeploying the previous image, which restores the old rail and paths together.

## EPIC Acceptance Criteria

### From Capability

- [AC-1] Every owner-interface screen MUST render inside one shell whose navigation groups Home, Research, Strategies and Portfolio apart from Data health and Settings, in that order, and a destination whose screen has not shipped MUST be absent rather than disabled.
- [AC-2] Until the first download has been started, the product MUST open on setup; afterwards it MUST open on Home.
- [AC-3] Going back to an earlier setup step MUST keep every value entered in later steps. This EPIC mounts `setup-ui`'s `SetupDraftProvider` above the `/setup` route so the drafts outlive route changes.
- [AC-10] Before paper trading ships, no unshipped destination MUST render, even disabled.
- [AC-16] The Data health destination MUST carry the number of followed stocks with problems and the Home destination the number of *Needs you* items, and each count MUST be absent when it is zero.
- [AC-17] Coverage, followed stocks, downloads, download detail and settings MUST open in the same shell, titled in the top bar, at `/data/coverage`, `/data/followed`, `/data/downloads`, `/data/downloads/:id` and `/settings`. This EPIC owns the route map and the titles.
- [AC-18] Each path CAP-001 shipped MUST take the owner to the same screen at its new path for one release after this capability ships.
- [AC-22] The owner MUST be able to open a command palette from the keyboard and from the top bar on every screen, and find followed stocks, universes and pages from it; it MUST NOT offer any action that places or stops trading.
- [AC-23] When the product stops answering, the main region MUST be replaced by the unreachable state while the rail and top bar remain; when it answers again the screen the owner was on MUST return without a reload.
- [AC-24] The shell's own states (loading, unreachable, not found, setup incomplete) and the palette's eight states MUST render as designed or record why one does not apply.
- [AC-25] Every operation MUST be completable by keyboard, and every region updated by polling MUST show its last-updated time, with the rail chips stood for by the Home and Data health *checked {time}* lines.
- [AC-26] The shell and palette MUST be built from `ui-components` and consume theme tokens only, with the approved-specification layout dimensions held in one layout-constants module.
- [AC-27] Replacing the interim Research entry CAP-002 appended to CAP-001's rail MUST leave every CAP-002 route unchanged and reachable from the new rail.
