# Register Home in the Shell: the /home Route, Its Title, the Home Destination and Its Chip

## User Story
As an owner-operator, I want Home in the rail with its Needs you count and reachable at `/home`, so that the product opens on it once my first download is requested.

## Description
The owner-shell story that closes E5 (DEC-0223 answer A): registers `/home` with `home-ui`'s `HomeScreen` in `web/src/app/routes.tsx`, adds *Home* to `titles.ts`, and adds `{ group: "primary", label: "Home", to: "/home", count: "needs-you" }` as the first entry of `DESTINATIONS` (owner-shell 2.6; DAC-E3-2: a destination enters only in the change that registers its screen), which makes the rail render the Home item with its count chip from `count-chip.tsx`. Extends `routes.test.tsx`, `titles.test.tsx` and `shell.test.tsx` (the Home item and chip, named *Home, {n} items need you*, absent at zero), extends `tests/e2e/startup-health.spec.ts` with the landing on Home after a download is requested and §2.10's latest-outcome assertion (Home's data tile shows the latest outcome), and extends `tests/e2e/shell-navigation.spec.ts` with the Home landing by rail and palette.

Source: component-design-owner-shell.md 2.6, 2.7, 2.10, 7; epic CAP-003-E3 DAC-E3-2, DAC-E3-7; epic CAP-003-E5 AC-16; DEC-0223.

## Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)

### From Capability / EPIC
- [AC-1] Home MUST be the first destination of the primary group.
- [AC-2] After the first download has been started, the product MUST open on Home: `/home` is now a registered route for `RootRedirect`.
- [AC-16] The Home destination MUST carry the number of *Needs you* items, absent when zero.
- [DAC-E3-2] A destination enters `DESTINATIONS` only in the change that registers its screen. (traces to: AC-1, AC-27)
- [DAC-E3-7] The Home chip MUST equal `deriveNeedsYou(attention, scores).length`, render no chip at zero, and name the count in words. (traces to: AC-16, AC-25)

### Derived
- [DAC-E5-S5-1] Given `DESTINATIONS` after this story, When the rail renders, Then Home precedes the Research entry (when registered), and the Pages group of the palette lists *Home*. (traces to: AC-1)
- [DAC-E5-S5-2] Given `startup-health.spec.ts`, When a download has been requested, Then `/` lands on `/home` with Home's `h1` visible and the data tile or its first-download sentence reflecting the latest download (owner-shell 2.10). (traces to: AC-2)
