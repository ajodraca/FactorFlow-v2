- **Observed evidence**: Each of these passes every home test, `token-literals.test.ts` and `tsc`:
  - removing `aria-hidden="true"` from the skeleton `ul` (`review-skeleton-aria-hidden`);
  - removing `min-h-7` and `animate-pulse` from the three rows (`review-skeleton-row-classes`);
  - removing `flex flex-col gap-3` from the `ul` (`review-skeleton-list-classes`).

  The loading assertions check only that the skeleton has three children and sits inside `aria-busy="true"`. Probe P1 passes on HEAD and fails on each mutant.
- **Expected evidence/behavior**: Plan T016: "`loading` → `<ul data-testid="needs-you-skeleton" aria-hidden="true" className="flex flex-col gap-3">` holding three `<li className="min-h-7 w-full animate-pulse rounded bg-secondary" />` … The classes are CAP-003-E5-S1's skeleton classes." home-ui §10: "Loading: skeleton header line, tile and three item rows." The rows' area then appears from nothing when attention answers, which the skeleton exists to prevent. - **Required remediation**: In T008's loading case, assert the skeleton's `aria-hidden="true"` and its `flex`, `flex-col` and `gap-3` classes. Also assert that each row has `min-h-7`, `w-full`, `animate-pulse`, `rounded` and `bg-secondary`. All three mutants must then fail.

### IR-003 — The empty state's `ok` guard is untested against a failed read

- **Status**: OPEN
