# Home

**EPIC ID:** CAP-003-E5
**Capability:** CAP-003
**Summary:** Give the owner one screen, `/home`, that says what needs them today and how current the data is in one look: a header with the date and market state, a data tile stating how many followed stocks are current out of how many are followed with the as-of times of scores and prices, and a *Needs you* list with the most important item first and exactly one navigating action each. Nothing on Home mutates state and Home never reads the expensive aggregate: it reads only the shell's attention and summary reads, so its tile badge is the worse of the Data health verdict tier, computed from the same problem set and latest terminal download Data health uses, and the score-state tier. Before any download is requested Home says no data has been downloaded yet and offers setup; from the first request until a download ends the tile gives no verdict and never *Yes*; scores that lag the latest download are said to lag rather than shown as current; and an item leaves the list only when its cause is gone. No strategy, paper or live region renders, and no placeholder for one.
**Actors:** Owner-operator; `home-ui`; `owner-shell` (`useShell`); `ui-components` (`deriveNeedsYou`, `sortAttention`, `worstDataTier`, `dataTileBadge`, `StatusBadge`, `NotDeterminable`, `Absent`, `AsOfLine`, formatters, `StateRegion`); `data-health-api` and `classification-api` through the shell.
**Estimate:** M
**Out of Scope:** The paper and live tiles, strategy items, the strategy list and *New strategy* (M2 onward, absent not placeholders); the Home rail count chip (E3, from the same derivation); the item derivation and sort themselves (E1); any read of `GET /api/data-health` (DEC-0219 withdrawn); a trading calendar, so the market state inside session hours is not determinable (system design open question 9); any action that mutates state.
**Dependencies:** E1 (the patterns listed above), E2 (`GET /api/attention` for the tile's prices as-of, followed count, problem set, latest and latest-terminal downloads and the server items) and E3 (the shell context, the `/home` route and the Home destination). Cross-capability: CAP-001 (`requires-runtime`) is reached only through E2's reads and the download detail links; CAP-002 (`requires-runtime`): the current count, the scores as-of, the lag badge and the two score-derived items read `GET /api/classifications/summary` as CAP-002 publishes it (DEC-0071; CAP-002 AC-12, AC-13, AC-20, AC-21, AC-22) and render not determinable while it answers `404`, so this EPIC is buildable and testable now against the published shape and its score regions are verified at integration if the read lands later (capability assumption 2). S01 and the blueprint (`conforms-to`) are designed against.
**Links:** system-design.md §3.3 (boxes 1 to 21 and notes 1 to 6), §4.1 `home-ui`, §4.2 (`Shell context` for `home-ui`, `GET /api/classifications/summary`), constraints 4, 5, 11; component-design-home-ui.md §2.1 to §2.5, §3, §7, §8, §10; component-design-ui-components.md §2.4, §2.6; ux-design.md §1.3, §0.8; `docs/ux/screens/S01-home.md` v4 (S01-3, S01-4, S01-6, S01-7).

## Goals

- Render the header's `h1` as *{Weekday}, {YYYY-MM-DD}* in America/Chicago with an as-of line of market state, *prices current to {prices_as_of}* and *checked {time}*.
- Render the data tile as one link to `/data` with a badge from `dataTileBadge`, the hero line *{current} of {followed} stocks current* with both figures from the summary's `stocks_current`, the scores and prices as-of lines, and the lag badge when scores do not reflect the latest download.
- Render *Needs you* as `sortAttention(deriveNeedsYou(attention, scores))` with the catalogue's copy per kind and one navigating action each, present exactly while its cause holds, with *Nothing needs you right now.* and *checked {time}* when empty.
- Render the honest states: the never-downloaded empty state with **Start setup** while `latest_download` is null, the first-download-running tile with no verdict while `latest_terminal_download` is null, not determinable for every figure whose source is not served, and the per-card error with **Try again** when a read fails.
- Render nothing for strategies, paper or live, leaving the summary row's other two columns empty.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **Freshness** (AC-25; STD-UI-25): every figure follows the shell's attention and summary cadence (60 s, 10 s while a download runs or scores build); *checked {time}* on the as-of line and the *Needs you* card is the attention read's `checked_at` as `HH:MM:SS zone` and ages while the read fails; the market state is recomputed each minute from the clock with no read.
- **Performance:** no read of its own; the list holds at most six items; the data item names at most three symbols and a count; the screen never waits on the coverage derivation (review v1 FINDING-4).
- **Accessibility** (AC-25; STD-UI-24): `main` with the date `h1`; each card a `section` labelled by its `h2`; the tile one link named by its title, tier and figures (or, in the first-download-running state, by its sentence); the list a `ul` of `li`s with the severity tile `aria-hidden` and the tag badge carrying the tier in words; a changed count announced politely through the card's live region without moving focus; the em dash carrying hidden *not determinable*.
- **Security:** `credential_present` reaches the screen only as the `credential_missing` item, never the key's value (AC-4); `failure_reason` and score `reason` are provider or engine text rendered as text (AGT-PROHIB-003); no storage, no request beyond the shell's reads.
- **Layout** (AC-26): main region capped at 1180 px from `layout.ts`; a three-column summary row with the tile in the first column; the 28 px severity tile from `layout.ts`; tokens only, with no `--gain` or `--loss`.

## EPIC Acceptance Criteria

### From Capability

- [AC-7] Home MUST show the first download's progress, through the *Your first download is running* item, until scores exist.
- [AC-8] Home MUST show how many followed stocks are current out of how many are followed, and the as-of time of scores and of prices, and MUST say when scores do not reflect the latest download.
- [AC-9] Every item on Home MUST have exactly one action, items MUST be ordered by severity tier then recency, and an item MUST leave the list only when its cause is resolved, never because it was viewed.
- [AC-10] Before paper trading ships, Home MUST render no strategy, paper or live region and no placeholder for one.
- [AC-12] Home MUST never present a partial or interrupted download as complete: the *download_not_complete* item renders *Interrupted* as serious and *Failed* as critical.
- [AC-16] Home's *Needs you* count MUST be the same derivation the rail's Home chip uses.
- [AC-21] Before any download has run, Home MUST state that no data has been downloaded yet and offer setup, rather than presenting empty checks as passed.
- [AC-24] Every Home region MUST render each of the eight states as designed or record why one does not apply.
- [AC-25] Every Home operation MUST be completable by keyboard, and every polled region MUST show its last-updated time.
- [AC-26] Home MUST be built from `ui-components` and consume theme tokens only.

### Derived

- [DAC-E5-1] While `attention.latest_download` is null Home MUST render one `card` with `h2` *No data has been downloaded yet.*, *Set up FactorFlow to name the stocks to follow and start the first download.* and **Start setup** (link to `/setup`), with the header still rendered and no tile or list. (traces to: AC-21)
- [DAC-E5-2] While `latest_terminal_download` is null and `latest_download` is not, the tile MUST render the first-download-running state: title *Data*, body *Your first download, started {YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM zone}, is running.* from `started_at` (or `requested_at` while queued), at most a `status-active` *Downloading* badge shown only while `latest_download.state` is `running`, and no verdict word, hero count, as-of line, lag badge or progress; `worstDataTier` and `dataTileBadge` return no answer in this state. (traces to: AC-21, AC-8)
- [DAC-E5-3] Once `latest_terminal_download` is set, the tile badge MUST be `dataTileBadge` over the verdict from attention's `problems.stock_count`, `followed_count` and `latest_terminal_download.state` and the summary's score state, so for the same inputs the tile's verdict tier equals `data-health-ui`'s verdict badge and the tile differs only when the score state is worse; no request to `/api/data-health` MUST be made. (traces to: AC-8, AC-11)
- [DAC-E5-4] The hero line MUST take `current_count` and `followed_count` both from the summary's `stocks_current` whenever the summary is served, so it never reads more current than followed; only while the summary is not served or carries no `stocks_current` MUST `followed` be attention's `followed_count` and the current count an em dash with hidden *not determinable*; the scores as-of MUST be the summary's `set.ended_at`, an em dash while not served and *No scores yet* when `never_classified`; the prices as-of MUST be attention's `prices_as_of` as a session date, unconverted. (traces to: AC-8)
- [DAC-E5-5] The lag badge `status-warning` *Not reflecting the latest download* MUST render when the summary's `set.not_current` is true or its `later` block reports a newer set building or failed, and never otherwise. (traces to: AC-8)
- [DAC-E5-6] The header MUST render the date as *{Weekday}, {YYYY-MM-DD}* (DEC-0209) and the market state as *Market closed* on Saturdays, Sundays and weekdays before 08:30 or from 15:00 America/Chicago, and as `coverage-unknown` *Market state not determinable* with *FactorFlow has no trading calendar, so it can't tell a session from a holiday.* inside weekday 08:30 to 15:00 (DEC-0206), across a DST change. (traces to: AC-24)
- [DAC-E5-7] Each item MUST render the catalogue's tag, tier, title, body and single action for its kind: `data_problems` (*Data*, warning, naming up to three symbols and *{k} more*, **Fix data** to `/data`), `download_not_complete` (*Download*, critical *The last download failed* or serious *The last download was interrupted*, **See download** to the detail), `credential_missing` (*Setup*, critical, **Open Settings** to `/settings`), `first_download` (*Download*, active, *{done} of {total} stocks so far.*, **See download**), `first_scores` (*Scores*, active, **See download**), `score_failed` (*Scores*, critical, **See details** to `/data`); plural forms MUST follow the count; `first_download` MUST drop its count clause while `total` is null; `score_failed` MUST drop its second sentence when no set exists. (traces to: AC-9)
- [DAC-E5-8] Every item action MUST be a link styled `button` `outline` whose accessible name names the item (*Fix data for KO, TSLA and FBXX*, *See the download that failed*, *Open Settings to set the market data key*); no action on Home MUST mutate state. (traces to: AC-9, AC-25)
- [DAC-E5-9] With no item, the card MUST read *Nothing needs you right now.* with *checked {time}*; a changed count MUST be announced politely and focus MUST never move. (traces to: AC-9, AC-25)
- [DAC-E5-10] No element with strategy, paper or live text or role MUST render; the summary row's second and third columns MUST be empty, with no *+ New strategy*, no *next rebalance* and no right-hand header action. (traces to: AC-10)
- [DAC-E5-11] When the attention read fails, the header MUST stay and each card's body MUST be replaced by *Couldn't read {what needs you / the data summary}: {reason}.* with **Try again**, with *checked {time}* aging; a summary error other than `404` MUST render inside the tile the same way; while the summary answers `404` the server items still render and the first-download item follows DEC-0216. (traces to: AC-24)
