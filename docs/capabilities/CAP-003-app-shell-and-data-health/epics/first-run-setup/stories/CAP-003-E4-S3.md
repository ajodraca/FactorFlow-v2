# Setup Step 3: Paste a List, the Paste Summary with Near-Miss Fixes, Batches and Add Later

## User Story
As an owner-operator, I want to paste a list of symbols and be told exactly which were accepted, duplicated or unreadable, so that one bad entry never costs me the good ones.

## Description
Creates `web/src/screens/setup/summarise-paste.ts` (`summarisePaste(parsed)` over the delivered `parseBulkSymbols` from `web/src/screens/instruments/parse-bulk-symbols.ts`, imported unchanged: `accepted` first occurrences, `duplicates` named once, `unreadable` each with a `fix` when replacing runs of slash, space or underscore between letters by one dot yields a valid symbol; DEC-0218), `post-in-batches.ts` (accepted symbols posted in order in batches of at most 1000, `MAX_SYMBOLS_PER_REQUEST` mirrored as a constant; a `409` batch treated as success; a transport failure or `500` keeping that batch's and later entries named in the textarea, counting the posted ones, leaving the step current) and `step-stocks.tsx` (step 3: `radio-group` `cards` with exactly *Paste a list* and *Add later*, `textarea` `list` parsed on paste and on blur with a polite parse counter, *Did you mean {fix}?* buttons, the below-30 warning with **Continue** available, **Continue** `blocked` with *Paste at least one symbol, or choose Add later.* at zero accepted with *Paste a list* chosen, already-followed entries reported from `duplicate_active` after Continue), with tests (setup-ui 2.1, 2.3, 10).

Source: component-design-setup-ui.md 2.1, 2.3, 4, 5, 7, 10; epic CAP-003-E4 DAC-E4-5 to DAC-E4-8, DAC-E4-11.
