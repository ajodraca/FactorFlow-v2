# Step 3: Stocks to Follow, the Paste Summary and Batched Posting

## User Story

As an owner-operator, I want to paste a list of symbols and see which were accepted, which were duplicates and which could not be read, with a warning when fewer than 30 are accepted, so that nothing I pasted is silently dropped and I can still continue.

## Description

Logical component: `setup-ui`. Creates `web/src/screens/setup/step-stocks.tsx`, `summarise-paste.ts` and `post-in-batches.ts` as `component-design-setup-ui.md` §2.3 and §10 specify. The `radio-group` `cards` offers exactly **Paste a list** and **Add later** (capability OQ-3, no file control). `summarisePaste` wraps the delivered `web/src/screens/instruments/parse-bulk-symbols.ts` unchanged and returns `accepted` (first occurrence), `duplicates` (named once, never posted twice) and `unreadable` (each with a `fix` when replacing runs of slash, space or underscore between letters by one dot yields a valid symbol; DEC-0218 PROVISIONAL), parsing on paste and on blur, never per keystroke. The counter *{a} symbols recognised, {d} duplicates, {r} not recognised* is a polite live region. **Continue** posts accepted symbols in order in batches of at most 1000 (`MAX_SYMBOLS_PER_REQUEST`, mirrored as a constant) through the delivered `addInstruments`; a `409` batch counts as success; a transport failure or `500` keeps that batch's and later entries in the textarea as `drafts.unposted`, counts the posted ones as followed and leaves the step current. Already-followed entries are reported after Continue from `duplicate_active`. Below 30 accepted the warning shows and **Continue** stays available; at zero accepted with *Paste a list* chosen **Continue** is `blocked`. *Add later* with **Continue** saves nothing and makes step 4 current (E4-S5 renders the blocked step 4).

Breakdown and ordering: DEC-0223.

## Technical Details

### API Contract

- Endpoint: `POST /api/instruments` (delivered, unchanged); Request: `{"symbols": [...]}`, at most 1000; Response: `201` with `added`, `reactivated`, `rejected` (with reasons) and the per-symbol dispositions; Errors: `409` every symbol already followed, `422` every entry malformed.
