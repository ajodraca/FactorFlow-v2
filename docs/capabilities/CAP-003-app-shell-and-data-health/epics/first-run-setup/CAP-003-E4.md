# First-Run Setup

**EPIC ID:** CAP-003-E4
**Capability:** CAP-003
**Summary:** Take the owner from an empty install to a first download in one visible multi-step operation at `/setup`, instead of three unrelated screens: step 1 states whether the market data key was found (never its value) and what to set when it is missing; step 2 saves the history depth; step 3 follows a pasted list of symbols, naming every duplicate and unreadable entry while keeping every accepted one and warning, without blocking, when fewer than 30 are accepted; step 4 starts the first download and shows its progress until the owner leaves. Every save and the start go through CAP-001's delivered endpoints, so the confirmations and refusals are the ones the owner already knows. Setup counts as complete once any download has been requested, read from the run journal with no stored flag; unsaved input and the values of later steps are held in app memory above the route for the session, never in browser storage, so going back and leaving keep them. *Add later* lets setup finish without a download, leaving step 4 blocked with two exits and `/` still opening setup.
**Actors:** Owner-operator; `setup-ui`; `owner-shell` (`useShell`, `SetupDraftProvider` placement); `ingestion-api` (`GET`/`PUT /api/settings/history-depth`, `GET`/`POST /api/instruments`, `POST /api/acquisition-runs`); `data-health-api` (`GET /api/attention`, through the shell).
**Estimate:** M
**Out of Scope:** Step 5, the paper trading account (M2); *Upload a CSV* as a way to name stocks (capability OQ-3, answered no); any change to the delivered `parse-bulk-symbols` function, which is imported unchanged; the Settings **Open setup** link (E7); the `/` redirect itself (E3); Home's rendering of the download's progress (E5); a scoped retry of failed stocks (blueprint D-9).
**Dependencies:** E1 (`stepper`, `card`, `badge`, `form`, `input`, `radio-group`, `textarea`, `progress`, `button`, `NotDeterminable`, formatters) and E3 (the shell context for `health.credential_present` and `attention.latest_download`, the `/setup` route and the mounting of this EPIC's `SetupDraftProvider` among the shell's providers). E2 for the progress figures in step 4 (`attention.latest_download.progress`); the step runs without them as indeterminate progress. Cross-capability: CAP-001 (`requires-runtime`) supplies every endpoint setup calls, the per-symbol dispositions, the `409` refusals for an unset depth or an empty followed list and the 1000-symbol request limit, all delivered (CAP-001 AC-1, AC-2, AC-19, AC-23); CAP-002 (`requires-runtime`) supplies only the 30-stock ranking floor the warning cites (CAP-002 AC-14), a constant, not a call. STD-UI-26 and S02 (`conforms-to`) are designed against.
**Links:** system-design.md §3.2 (steps 1 to 22 and notes 1 to 6), §4.1 `setup-ui`, §4.2 (`Shell context` for `setup-ui`, `GET`/`PUT /api/settings/history-depth`, `POST /api/instruments`, `GET /api/instruments`, `POST /api/acquisition-runs`), §4.4, constraints 2, 10; component-design-setup-ui.md §2.1 to §2.3, §3, §4, §7, §8, §10; ux-design.md §1.2, §0.8; `docs/ux/screens/S02-setup.md` v4 (S02-1 to S02-6).

## Goals

- Show four steps as done, current or to do, opened on the first step not done, with each done flag re-derived from the server on every visit: health has answered, years set, `active_count` above 0, a download requested.
- Keep the years text, the source choice, the pasted text and any unposted entries in `SetupDraftProvider` for the session, so going back, following a done-step link, or leaving `/setup` and returning never discards unsaved input.
- State key presence as *Found* or *Missing* with the variable name and the restart line when missing, *Not determinable* while health is unreachable, and never the value.
- Parse a pasted list on paste and on blur with the delivered parser, name duplicates once and unreadable entries with a near-miss fix where one exists, keep every accepted entry whatever else is rejected, and post accepted symbols in batches of at most 1000 with each batch's dispositions reported.
- Warn below 30 accepted symbols and still let the owner continue; block **Continue** with its reason only at zero accepted with *Paste a list* chosen, offering *Add later*.
- Start the first download through the delivered endpoint, show its progress from the shell's attention read with *checked {time}*, say when it is queued behind a running one, and name the step that fixes each refusal.
- After *Add later*, show step 4 blocked with CAP-001's reason and two exits, **Go to Home** and **Manage followed stocks**, with setup still incomplete.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **Freshness and cadence** (AC-25; STD-UI-25): step 4's progress follows the shell's attention read at 10 s while the download is non-terminal and shows *checked {time}* from the read's `checkedAt`; this screen opens no interval of its own; the progress bar is announced at the 10 second cadence, never per frame.
- **Performance:** a 3000-symbol paste is parsed once on paste and once on blur, never per keystroke, and posted in three requests of at most 1000 (`MAX_SYMBOLS_PER_REQUEST`, mirrored as a constant).
- **Accessibility** (AC-25; STD-UI-24, STD-UI-26): the step list is a `nav` *Setup steps* holding an `ol` with `aria-current="step"` and done steps saying so in text; the step card is a `section` labelled by its `h2`, which takes focus when the step changes; the radio group is one tab stop with arrow keys; the parse counter is a polite live region tied to the textarea; rejected entries are announced with their reason; the years suffix is part of the input's accessible name; `button` `blocked` stays focusable with its reason described.
- **Security** (AC-4; SEC-INP-001): no secret is handled, presence is a boolean; pasted text is parsed locally and posted as a JSON array the delivered route validates per symbol; nothing is written to browser storage; server reasons render as text.
- **Layout** (AC-26): two columns inside the 1180 px main region, the left 260 to 300 px, the 28 px step dot and the 70ch explanation measure read from `owner-shell`'s `layout.ts`; tokens only otherwise.
- **Operations:** new screen, no migration; a page reload re-derives saved steps and loses unsaved drafts, which S02 §7 accepts.

## EPIC Acceptance Criteria

### From Capability

- [AC-2] Starting the first download from setup MUST make `/` open on Home from then on; until a download has been requested, including after *Add later*, `/` MUST keep opening setup.
- [AC-3] Setup MUST show its steps, the owner's position and what remains, and going back to an earlier step MUST keep every value entered in later steps.
- [AC-4] Setup MUST state whether the market data credential was found and, when it is missing, which environment variable to set, and MUST NOT display its value.
- [AC-5] When the owner pastes a list of symbols, the product MUST accept the valid entries, name each entry it could not read and each duplicate, and MUST NOT discard an accepted entry because another was rejected.
- [AC-6] When fewer than 30 symbols are accepted, setup MUST say that no factor can be ranked below that count and MUST still let the owner continue.
- [AC-7] The owner MUST be able to start the first download from setup and leave; the download's progress MUST show in setup while the owner stays.
- [AC-17] Saving the history depth, following stocks and starting a download MUST go through the delivered endpoints with CAP-001's confirmations and refusals unchanged.
- [AC-21] After *Add later*, setup MUST let the owner leave with no download started, **Go to Home** landing on Home's honest empty state.
- [AC-24] Every setup region MUST render each of the eight states as designed or record why one does not apply.
- [AC-25] Every setup operation MUST be completable by keyboard, and the polled progress MUST show its last-updated time.
- [AC-26] Setup MUST be built from `ui-components` and consume theme tokens only.
