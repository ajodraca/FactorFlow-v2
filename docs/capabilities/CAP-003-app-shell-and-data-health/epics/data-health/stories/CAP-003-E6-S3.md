# *Needs Fixing*: Problem Rows, Paging and the Nothing-Wrong State

## User Story

As an owner-operator, I want one row per followed stock with a problem naming what was being downloaded, what failed, why, what it means and the fix that applies, paged as I ask for more, so that every counted stock has a row and every row has a fix.

## Description

Logical component: `data-health-ui`. Creates `web/src/screens/data-health/needs-fixing.tsx` (header `h2` *Needs fixing · {n}* from `affected_count`, the sub-line *From your most recent downloads · checked {time}*, the rows, **Show more**, and the nothing-wrong state) and `problem-row.tsx` (the row composition of §2.5), as `component-design-data-health-ui.md` §2.5 specifies. Rows come from the aggregate's first page of 50 and **Show more** appends `fetchProblems(cursor, 50)` (E3-S1) while `next_cursor` is set. The header's **Download now** control and the per-row **Stop following** and **Follow a different symbol** controls are E6-S4's; this story renders their slots with the row's fix text and a `copy.ts` placeholder until E6-S4 lands. When `affected_count` is 0 the card reads *Nothing needs fixing.* with *Last complete download {start time}* from the delivered `listAcquisitionRuns({ state: "succeeded", limit: 1 })`, or *No complete download yet*.

Breakdown and ordering: DEC-0223.
