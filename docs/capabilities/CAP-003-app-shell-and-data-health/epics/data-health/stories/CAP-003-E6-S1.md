# Data Health Screen Scaffold, Verdict and Empty States

## User Story

As an owner-operator, I want Data health to open with one sentence that says whether the data can be trusted, and to say plainly that nothing has been downloaded when that is the case, so that the answer is honest before I read anything else.

## Description

Logical component: `data-health-ui`. Creates `web/src/screens/data-health/index.tsx` (`DataHealthScreen`: the `h1` *Is the data good enough to trust?*, the verdict under it, **Full coverage table** and **Download now** right of it, the four-column check-card grid with the fourth empty, the *Needs fixing* slot and the two lower columns), `verdict.tsx` and `copy.ts`, as `component-design-data-health-ui.md` §2.1 to §2.3 specify. The badge is E1-S3's `worstDataTier` over the aggregate's `affected_count` and `followed_count` from `useDataHealth()` and attention's `latest_terminal_download.state` from `useShell()`; the sentences are §2.3's table (DEC-0210, DEC-0217 as amended, PROVISIONAL). The aggregate is read on load, on **Try again**, once when the attention read shows the latest download turned terminal, and after **Stop following** (E6-S4), never on a timer (E3-S2's hook). While `latest_terminal_download` is null the screen renders the never-downloaded card in place of the verdict, the check cards and What you follow; a download that ended having stored nothing (`never_acquired` true) renders the verdict with the check cards' null state instead (review v1 FINDING-10). Cards (E6-S2), rows (E6-S3), actions (E6-S4) and the lower columns (E6-S5) fill their slots later; **Download now** here renders E6-S4's component once it lands and a placeholder from `copy.ts` until then. The `/data` route is registered by E3-S9.

Carries AC-19 and AC-20 for this screen (review v1 FINDING-4): every string lives in `copy.ts` in the display vocabulary; statuses render only through `StatusBadge` and `NotDeterminable`.

Breakdown and ordering: DEC-0223.

- [DAC-E6-2] While attention loads the verdict MUST render a skeleton, and while attention fails the verdict region MUST keep its header and render *Couldn't read what needs attention: {reason}.* with **Try again**, computing no tier without the download input. (traces to: AC-11, AC-24)
