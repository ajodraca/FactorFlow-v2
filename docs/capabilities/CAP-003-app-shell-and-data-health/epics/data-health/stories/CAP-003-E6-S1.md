# The Verdict and the Three Check Cards

## User Story
As an owner-operator, I want Data health to open with one sentence saying whether the data can be trusted and three cards saying what each check found, so that I decide in one look whether to act.

## Description
Creates `web/src/screens/data-health/copy.ts`, `verdict.tsx` (badge from `worstDataTier` over the aggregate's `affected_count` and `followed_count` and attention's `latest_terminal_download.state`, the sentence composed per data-health-ui 2.3 with the badge as its first clause, singular forms, the download clause replacing the count clause's full stop, a not-determinable check named after the sentence, *checked {time}*, **Full coverage table** and **Download now** right of the `h1`; a skeleton while attention loads; *Couldn't read what needs attention: {reason}.* with **Try again** while it fails, no tier computed), `check-card.tsx` (the card composition of UX design §3), `daily-prices-card.tsx`, `financials-card.tsx` and `scores-card.tsx` per data-health-ui 2.4 (result lines omitting zero parts and never showing a null as `0`, up to five exception symbols with *and {k} more* from `exception_count` minus the shown, the STD-UI-18 scope notes, the Factor scores card from `useShell().scores` with *{k} stocks show as Incomplete* from `stocks_current` alone, *Scores {time} · from the download started {start time}* with the start time a link). Writes `verdict.test.tsx` and `check-cards.test.tsx`.

Source: component-design-data-health-ui.md 2.1, 2.3, 2.4, 3, 7; epic CAP-003-E6 DAC-E6-1 to DAC-E6-5; DEC-0210, DEC-0217 as amended.

## Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)

### From Capability / EPIC
- [AC-11] Data health MUST open with a one-sentence verdict naming how many followed stocks need attention, and check cards for daily prices, company financials and factor scores, each carrying its severity tier and the time it is current to.
- [AC-8] The Factor scores card MUST read the shell's summary.
- [AC-24] A null part MUST never show as `0`; a not-determinable check MUST render as such.
- [DAC-E6-1] The verdict badge and sentences MUST be as stated; a check that is not determinable MUST be named after the sentence. (traces to: AC-11)
- [DAC-E6-2] While attention loads the verdict MUST render a skeleton, and while attention fails its header MUST stay with the error and **Try again**, computing no tier. (traces to: AC-11, AC-24)
- [DAC-E6-3] The Daily prices card MUST render as stated. (traces to: AC-11, AC-24)
- [DAC-E6-4] The Company financials card MUST render as stated. (traces to: AC-11)
- [DAC-E6-5] The Factor scores card MUST read the shell's summary as stated. (traces to: AC-11, AC-8)

### Derived
- [DAC-E6-S1-1] Given `affected_count` 1 of 10 with a `partial` latest download, When rendered, Then the sentence is *Mostly yes. 1 of 10 followed stocks needs attention; its scores show as Incomplete until it's fixed.*; given `affected_count` 10 of 10, Then `status-critical` *No*; given a `failed` latest download with 0 affected, Then *No. None of the 10 followed stocks needs attention, and the last download failed.* (traces to: DAC-E6-1)
- [DAC-E6-S1-2] Given `gap_stock_count` null with `gap_detection.reason`, When the Daily prices card renders, Then the gap part reads *Gaps not determinable: {reason}* as `coverage-unknown` while the absent and behind parts still render, and the verdict names *Daily prices can't be checked for gaps: {reason}.* (traces to: DAC-E6-3)
- [DAC-E6-S1-3] Given `exception_count` 7 with five symbols shown, When rendered, Then *and 2 more* follows; given 5, Then no *and {k} more* (DEC-0220). (traces to: DAC-E6-3)
- [DAC-E6-S1-4] Given the verdict and the three check cards in every state rendered in every state this story defines, When its visible text and accessible names are scanned for *instrument*, *acquisition*, *acquire*, *run*, *runs*, *Retire*, *Retired*, *Provider credential* as whole words in any case (the DAC-E7-3 term list), Then none appears, and every label is the STD-UI-1 v6 display label from `labelOf` or this component's `copy.ts`, never the domain term beside it. (traces to: AC-19)
- [DAC-E6-S1-5] Given the verdict badge, each check's tier badge (`ok` good, `exceptions` warning) and the score-state badge through `StatusBadge`, *Not determinable* through `NotDeterminable`, When rendered, Then each goes through `StatusBadge` with its label as text and its tier shape `aria-hidden`, a value no artifact can determine goes through `NotDeterminable`, and no state is conveyed by colour alone. (traces to: AC-20)
