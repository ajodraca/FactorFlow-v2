# Register Data Health in the Shell: the /data Route, Its Title, the Data Health Destination and Its Chip

## User Story
As an owner-operator, I want Data health in the rail with its problem count and reachable at `/data`, so that the count and the screen it opens never disagree.

## Description
The owner-shell story that closes E6 (DEC-0223 answer A): registers `/data` with `data-health-ui`'s `DataHealthScreen` in `web/src/app/routes.tsx` (the parent of the already-registered `/data/*` screens, whose *Data health* crumb now resolves), adds *Data health* to `titles.ts`, and adds `{ group: "data", label: "Data health", to: "/data", count: "problems" }` to `DESTINATIONS` before Settings (owner-shell 2.6; DAC-E3-2), which makes the rail render the Data health item with its chip from `count-chip.tsx` (`attention.problems.stock_count`, named *Data health, {n} stocks with problems*, absent at zero). Extends `routes.test.tsx`, `titles.test.tsx` and `shell.test.tsx`, extends `tests/e2e/shell-navigation.spec.ts` with the Data health landing by rail and palette, and, with Home and Data health both registered, the palette's Pages group lists every shipped destination.

Source: component-design-owner-shell.md 2.6, 2.7, 7; epic CAP-003-E3 DAC-E3-2, DAC-E3-7; epic CAP-003-E6 AC-16; DEC-0223.

## Acceptance Criteria (Given/When/Then)

### From Capability / EPIC
- [AC-1] Data health MUST sit in the data-and-system group before Settings.
- [AC-16] The Data health destination MUST carry the number of followed stocks with problems, absent when zero.
- [AC-17] Data health MUST open in the same shell, titled in the top bar.
- [DAC-E3-2] A destination enters `DESTINATIONS` only in the change that registers its screen. (traces to: AC-1, AC-27)
- [DAC-E3-7] The Data health chip MUST equal `attention.problems.stock_count`, render no chip at zero, name the count in words and keep its last value marked stale while attention fails. (traces to: AC-16, AC-25)

### Derived
- [DAC-E6-S7-1] Given `/data/downloads` after this story, When the top bar renders, Then the *Data health* crumb links to `/data` and lands on the Data health screen. (traces to: AC-17)
- [DAC-E6-S7-2] Given `shell-navigation.spec.ts`, When Data health is chosen from the rail and from the palette's Pages group by keyboard, Then `/data` renders with its `h1` focused. (traces to: AC-1, AC-25)
