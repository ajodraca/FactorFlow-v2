# The Three Check Cards: Daily Prices, Company Financials and Factor Scores

## User Story

As an owner-operator, I want each data check on its own card with one tier, its result and the time it is current to, so that I can see which kind of data has a problem and that a check which could not run is shown as not determinable rather than passed.

## Description

Logical component: `data-health-ui`. Creates `web/src/screens/data-health/check-card.tsx` (the composition of §10: name as `h2`, tier badge, bold result line, as-of and exceptions line), `daily-prices-card.tsx`, `financials-card.tsx` and `scores-card.tsx`, as `component-design-data-health-ui.md` §2.4 specifies. Daily prices and Company financials read the aggregate's `checks` (DEC-0201, DEC-0213, DEC-0220, PROVISIONAL); Factor scores reads `useShell().scores`. Zero parts of a result line are omitted and a null part is never shown as `0`; *and {k} more* takes `exception_count` minus the symbols shown and is omitted at 0; the Daily prices scope note is STD-UI-18's sentence with **Full coverage table**. The null state of E6-S1 (DAC-E6-7) is rendered by `check-card.tsx` when its check is `null`.

Breakdown and ordering: DEC-0223.

- [AC-11] Check cards for daily prices, company financials and factor scores MUST each carry their severity tier and the time they are current to.
- [AC-12] No region MUST present a check built on a partial or interrupted download as complete.
- [AC-8] The Factor scores card MUST say when scores do not reflect the latest download.
- [AC-24] A check that could not run MUST render as not determinable with its reason.
- [AC-20] Every check result MUST render through `StatusBadge` with label and shape.
- [DAC-E6-3] The Daily prices card MUST render `ok` as *No missing prices or gaps* (good), `exceptions` as *Needs attention* (warning) and `not_determinable` as `coverage-unknown` *Not determinable*; its result line MUST be *All {m} stocks have prices to {latest_session}* or the non-zero parts of *{absent_count} without prices, {gap_stock_count} with gaps, {behind_count} behind {latest_session}*, with a null `gap_stock_count` rendered as *Gaps not determinable: {reason}* while the other parts still render; its as-of line MUST carry *Prices to {latest_session}*, up to five `exception_symbols`, *and {k} more* with *k* = `exception_count` minus the symbols shown (omitted at 0), and the STD-UI-18 scope note with **Full coverage table**. (traces to: AC-11, AC-24)
- [DAC-E6-4] The Company financials card MUST render `ok` as *All financials present* and `exceptions` as *Needs attention*, the result line *All {m} stocks have financials* or *{absent_count} without financials, {problem_count} with failed downloads* with zero parts omitted, *Financials to {as_of}* with the zone, its exceptions and the note *Checks that financials are present and downloaded, not that they are correct.* (traces to: AC-11)
- [DAC-E6-5] The Factor scores card MUST read the shell's summary: `coverage-unknown` *Not determinable* with *FactorFlow can't read scores yet.* while not served; *No scores yet* (neutral), *Updating* (active), *Score update failed* (critical), *Not reflecting the latest download* (warning) or *Current* (good) per the summary's state; the result line *{current} of {followed} stocks current* from `stocks_current` with *{k} stocks show as Incomplete until their data is complete* where *k* is `stocks_current.followed_count` minus `current_count`; and *Scores {time} · from the download started {start time}* with the start time a link to the download detail.
