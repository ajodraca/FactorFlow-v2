# Data Health

**EPIC ID:** CAP-003-E6
**Capability:** CAP-003
**Summary:** Answer *is the data good enough to trust?* in one sentence at `/data`, and name for every followed stock with a problem what was being downloaded, what failed, why, what it means and the fix that applies. The screen opens with a verdict composed from the one problem set and the latest terminal download, three check cards (Daily prices, Company financials, Factor scores) each with its tier, result and the time it is current to, a *Needs fixing* list paged by keyset, the five most recent downloads with a running one's progress at its head, and a summary of what the owner follows. It offers the two recoveries the delivered API supports and no other: **Download now**, a full download over the followed list that CAP-001 makes safe to repeat, accepted and queued when one is already running; and, for a symbol the provider does not recognise, **Stop following** (confirmed, history kept) and **Follow a different symbol**, never a download. The expensive aggregate is read on demand only, and once more when a running download ends so the checks refresh.
**Actors:** Owner-operator; `data-health-ui`; `owner-shell` (`useShell`, `useDataHealth`, `fetchProblems`, `refresh`); `data-health-api` (`GET /api/data-health`, `GET /api/data-health/problems`, `GET /api/attention` through the shell); `ingestion-api` (`GET`/`POST /api/acquisition-runs`, `GET /api/instruments`, `DELETE /api/instruments/{symbol}`, `GET /api/settings/history-depth`); `classification-api` (summary, through the shell).
**Estimate:** L
**Out of Scope:** The *Live prices* check card (M3), the schedule line under *Recent downloads* and the broker row in *What you follow* (M2), none reserved; a download scoped to the stocks that failed (blueprint D-9); strategy wording in the consequence and verdict (M2; DEC-0210); the Data health rail chip (E3) and the aggregate's computation (E2); the full coverage table, the followed-stocks screen and the download detail these links open (E7); the Stale state on the check cards, which returns when a trading calendar exists (system design open question 9).
**Dependencies:** E1 (`card`, `badge`, `button`, `dialog` `confirm`, `data-table`, `progress`, `StatusBadge`, `NotDeterminable`, `Absent`, `worstDataTier`, formatters, `StateRegion`), E2 (the three reads) and E3 (the shell context, `useDataHealth`, the `/data` route and the Data health destination). Cross-capability: CAP-001 (`requires-runtime`) supplies every write this screen makes and the run list, retire and instrument endpoints, all delivered (CAP-001 AC-6 makes **Download now** a safe retry; AC-10, AC-11 the unrecognised-symbol diagnosis; AC-19, AC-23, AC-24 starting and queueing; AC-21, AC-25 stopping following with history kept). CAP-002 (`requires-runtime`): the Factor scores card reads the published summary and renders not determinable while it answers `404`, verified at integration if that read lands later (capability assumption 2). S09, the blueprint §9 and STD-UI-2, STD-UI-18, STD-UI-19 (`conforms-to`) are designed against.
**Links:** system-design.md §3.4 (steps 1 to 36 and notes 1 to 8), §4.1 `data-health-ui`, §4.2 (`Shell context` for `data-health-ui`, the `ingestion-api` rows consumed by `data-health-ui`), constraints 4, 5, 6, 7, 8; component-design-data-health-ui.md §2.1 to §2.7, §3, §4, §7, §8, §10; component-design-ui-components.md §2.4; ux-design.md §1.4, §0.8; `docs/ux/screens/S09-data-health.md` v4 (S09-1 to S09-5).

## Goals

- Compose the verdict from `worstDataTier` over `affected_count`, `followed_count` and attention's `latest_terminal_download`, with the badge as the sentence's first clause and the M1 sentences of system design constraint 4.
- Render the three check cards with one tier each, result lines that omit zero parts and never show a null as `0`, as-of lines and up to five exception symbols with *and {k} more*, the gap part as not determinable with its reason when gap detection could not run, and the Factor scores card from the shell's summary.
- Render *Needs fixing* as one row per problem stock, ordered by symbol, naming the stock, each failure's data type, outcome, reason and attempts, the consequence, and the fix that applies; **Download now** in the header only when a row is retryable; **Show more** appending pages of 50.
- Offer **Download now** as one routine action behind `POST /api/acquisition-runs`, **Stop following** behind a confirmed `DELETE /api/instruments/{symbol}`, and **Follow a different symbol** as a link to the followed-stocks screen with its add field focused.
- Read the aggregate on load, on **Try again**, once when the latest download turns terminal and after **Stop following**, never on an interval; re-read Recent downloads on each 10 s attention tick while a download runs.
- Render the honest states: the never-downloaded empty card while no download has ended, the check cards' null state when a download ended having stored nothing, *Nothing needs fixing.* with the last complete download when nothing is wrong.

## Non-Functional Requirements

- **Latency and load** (AC-28; system design §3.4 note 1): one aggregate read per visit plus one per download end and one per stop; the aggregate is never polled; Recent downloads holds at most five rows and is re-read at 10 s only while a download runs; *Needs fixing* pages by 50 under the canonical bounds, so 300 problem stocks are six pages (STD-UI-30).
- **Freshness** (AC-25; STD-UI-25): *checked {time}* under the verdict and in the *Needs fixing* sub-line from the aggregate's `checked_at`; the Recent downloads header carries *checked {time}*, the time the list last answered, both while a download runs and otherwise.
- **Accessibility** (AC-25; STD-UI-24): each card a `section` labelled by its `h2`; the verdict one sentence to a screen reader; rows list items whose actions name the symbol; the table with the start time as row header; the running `progressbar` labelled by its text; the dialog `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal`, focus trapped, cancel first and focused, focus returned to the row's **Stop following** or, when the row has left, to the *Needs fixing* heading; polling and a row leaving announce politely and never move focus; greyscale rendering keeps every tier distinguishable (STD-UI-8).
- **Security** (AC-4; S09-4): the key's value never appears; reasons render as text; the two writes are the delivered `POST /api/acquisition-runs` and `DELETE /api/instruments/{symbol}` behind the delivered Origin check; no storage; no `request_id` is shown.
- **Action tiers** (STD-UI-19; blueprint §9): downloading is routine with no confirmation; stopping following is consequential, confirmed with the blueprint's wording and `dialog` `confirm`, never `destructive`, because history is kept (STD-UI-2).
- **Layout** (AC-26): reading width capped at 1180 px from `layout.ts`; a four-column check-card grid with the fourth empty and no placeholder; tokens only.
