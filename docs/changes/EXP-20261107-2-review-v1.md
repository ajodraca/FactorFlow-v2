## Test and Validation Verification

**The regression test, read.** `test_the_client_runs_no_classification_scheduler` asserts that `app_module.run_classification_loop is _idle_scheduler` while the `client` fixture is active. That pins the fixture's configuration, not the absence of a set. The other link in the chain, that the lifespan starts whatever `app_module.run_classification_loop` names, is pinned by `tests/unit/test_api_classification_lifespan.py` (the TC-COMP-087 recording-loop test, which `setattr`s the same attribute and asserts it was called once with `pool` and `settings`). The race needs a claim between two autocommit statements, and a deterministic reproduction would need a hook in `seed_set` or the scheduler.
